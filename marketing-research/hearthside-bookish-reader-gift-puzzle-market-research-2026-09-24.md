# Hearthside Puzzles — Bookish & Reader-Gift Puzzle Market Research

**Research date:** September 24, 2026  
**Business target:** **$10,000/month Etsy revenue**  
**Channel:** Etsy  
**Fulfillment:** Printify / Printful POD  
**Brand:** Hearthside Puzzles — *Cozy puzzles for slow evenings in.*


## Executive recommendation

**Bookish / reader-gift is the strongest niche expansion for Hearthside.** It aligns almost perfectly with the existing brand and shows clear Etsy demand across both broad and narrow searches.

Public Etsy pages currently show:
- `book lover puzzle`: **3,000+ items**
- `reading nook puzzle`: **745+ items**
- `bookstore puzzle`: about **403+ items** on the sampled page
- `cozy bookstore puzzle`: only **133+ items**

The key insight is that **broad book-lover demand is crowded, while highly specific cozy book environments are much narrower**. Hearthside should therefore lead with visual-scene phrases such as `cozy bookstore puzzle`, `reading nook puzzle`, `library puzzle`, and `book cafe puzzle`, then layer `book lover gift` and `bookish puzzle` into titles/tags.

## Etsy market signal

Visible examples show multiple commercially useful patterns:

- A **Bookworm Life 500-piece bookshelf puzzle** is listed at about **$24** and shows very high shop/listing review signal.
- A **Cozy Cafe Reading Nook** puzzle is around **$31.99**.
- A **Vintage Book Lover** 110/252/520/1014 puzzle is around **$21.99**.
- `cozy bookstore puzzle` includes premium examples at **$34.60**, **$45**, **$46.65 before sale**, and wooden versions above $50.
- Etsy review snippets explicitly mention buying book-themed puzzles as gifts for parents, partners, and friends who love both books and puzzles.

This is not proof of exact monthly sales, but it is strong evidence that the buyer intersection exists and supports multiple price points.

## Customer profile

**Primary buyer**
- reader, librarian, teacher, book-club member, BookTok / bookish shopper, bookstore lover
- buys for self and gifts
- already likes the exact Hearthside props: books, mugs, lamps, rain, cats, blankets, plants

**Emotional job**
> “Give me a puzzle that feels like the room I wish I could read in.”

## Best sub-niches for Hearthside

### 1. Reading Nooks
Most direct brand fit. High emotional clarity at thumbnail size.

### 2. Independent Bookshops
Narrower public competition than broad book-lover terms and strong gift appeal.

### 3. Home Libraries
More premium / aspirational. Excellent for frame-worthy finished art.

### 4. Book Cafés
Adds food, windows, tableware, rain, and street detail for richer puzzle assembly.

### 5. Cat + Books
Use the orange tabby as a recurring supporting character rather than a novelty-cat concept.

## Pricing read

The niche spans bargain POD listings through premium wooden and keepsake puzzles. Hearthside should sit above the low-price commodity range because the art is the differentiator.

Recommended:
- 520-piece standard: **$36.95**
- 1014-piece standard: **$54.95**
- Test **$39.95 / $56.95** on proven bookshop/library listings with strong gift packaging and mockups.

## POD product architecture

### Primary production SKU — Printify

**Product:** Puzzle (110, 252, 520, 1014-piece)  
**Provider:** District Photo  
**Current catalog signal:** Printify Bestseller; District Photo score about **9.4** at research time  
**Material / finish:** 100% chipboard, glossy laminated finish  
**Packaging:** paper box with artwork on top; reference print included  
**Primary launch size:** **520 pieces, horizontal — 20.00 × 15.98 in**  
**Premium size:** **1014 pieces, horizontal — 28.50 × 19.02 in**  
**Catalog starting price:** from **$8.39** before variant-specific pricing; verify the exact 520/1014 cost immediately before publishing.

**Why this is the primary Hearthside product:** the 520-piece horizontal size is essentially a 5:4 canvas and fits Hearthside's existing art system with minimal compromise. The 1014-piece variant gives us an adult premium tier in the same product family. The paper box is giftable, and the product includes a reference print.

### Backup / quality-comparison SKU — Printful

**Product:** Jigsaw Puzzle  
**Variant:** **520 pieces — 19.74 × 15.74 in**  
**Material:** 1.53 mm pressed paper chipboard  
**Finish:** semi-gloss  
**Current listed base price:** from **$15.25**  
**US shipping:** from **$5.89**  
**Availability:** currently US only

Use Printful as a controlled sample comparison / fallback supplier rather than the first choice. Its current product page shows strong artwork reproduction but mixed customer comments about piece fit, so physical sampling remains mandatory.

### Default Hearthside pricing

| Variant | Normal price | Promo / launch band | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 520-piece | **$36.95** | **$32.95–$34.95** | Core adult puzzle |
| 1014-piece | **$54.95** | **$49.95–$52.95** | Premium / longer build |

For highly giftable **bookish or dark-academia** designs, test **$39.95 / $56.95** as a premium-price experiment after the listing proves demand. Do not use permanent 40–50% discounting as the store's default merchandising strategy.

### Artwork deliverables

- **520-piece master:** horizontal **5:4**, target **5000 × 4000 px or larger**.
- **1014-piece premium master:** horizontal **3:2**, target **6000 × 4000 px or larger**.
- Extend the 5:4 scene laterally for the 1014 version; do not crop away the focal point.
- Keep 5–7% safe breathing room around critical objects.
- Fit to the exact live provider template before production.

## Recommended first PRD concepts

The first Hearthside production sprint already covers a rainy reading nook, hidden bookshop, and library hearth. This niche PRD therefore adds **new sibling scenes** rather than repeating those designs:

1. **Rainy Book Café Window** — book lover + café + rain.
2. **Upstairs Used Bookshop** — intimate, layered, slightly vintage, strong detail density.
3. **Sunday Home Library Table** — aspirational home-library scene centered on a communal reading table.

## What to avoid

- copyrighted book covers, series references, or fandom shorthand
- generated readable titles on book spines
- fantasy libraries that drift away from believable Hearthside rooms
- shelves that become undifferentiated brown noise
- too many tiny books with no larger visual landmarks
- “BookTok” as the entire concept rather than a discoverability keyword

## Decision

**Make Bookish / Reader Gift a permanent customer lane and likely the first niche to scale after the initial three-week pipeline.** The combination of broad audience, narrow scene-based search opportunities, giftability, and perfect brand fit is stronger than any other niche reviewed in this package.
