**Research date:** September 24, 2026  
**Business target:** **$10,000/month Etsy revenue**  
**Channel:** Etsy

**Brand:** Hearthside Puzzles — *Cozy puzzles for slow evenings in.*


## Executive recommendation

**Hygge is an attractive low-competition identity keyword, but it should not be used alone as the commercial market.** Etsy currently shows only about **130+ items** for `hygge puzzle`, while the broader `cabin puzzle` page shows **2,000+ items**. The best strategy is therefore to combine **hygge as the emotional/aesthetic language** with broader concrete search terms such as `cozy cabin puzzle`, `winter cabin puzzle`, `lake cabin puzzle`, `fireside puzzle`, and `cozy winter puzzle`.

This may be the niche that most naturally expresses Hearthside's tagline: **cozy puzzles for slow evenings in.**

## Etsy market signal

- `hygge puzzle`: about **130+ items** — low exact-match competition, but likely lower search volume too.
- Visible results include a **Nordic winter village 500/1000-piece puzzle around $47.99 sale price**, a **Norwegian folk-art village puzzle around $24.75**, a **cozy cabin autumn 500/1000-piece puzzle around $36.24**, and a **cozy winter fireplace living-room puzzle around $22.58**.
- `cabin puzzle`: **2,000+ items** on a sampled Etsy page, with visible cozy mountain-cabin, snowy-cabin, lake-house, autumn-cabin, and lodge designs.
- Cabin listings regularly use gift language for nature lovers, families, lake-house owners, and winter shoppers.

## Customer profile

**Primary buyer**
- homebody, cabin / lake-house lover, winter-cozy shopper, couple, nature lover
- likes wool, wood, fireplaces, candles, hot drinks, books, simple rustic interiors
- buys especially strongly in fall/winter but can be reached year-round with lake and rain variants

**Emotional job**
> “Give me the feeling of being tucked inside while the weather happens outside.”

## Best sub-niches for Hearthside

### 1. Snowed-In Cabin Interior
The clearest winter conversion concept. Use snow as exterior texture, not a blank field.

### 2. Lake Cabin Slow Evening
Evergreen version with rain, dusk, canoe, dock, books, mugs, plaid, and firelight.

### 3. Nordic Breakfast / Supper Room
Warm natural materials, candles, simple ceramics, bread, wool, divided windows. Avoid sterile minimalist Scandinavian styling.

### 4. Cabin Craft / Knitting Room
Strong hygge activity cues: wool, basket, chair, tea, fire, window weather. Good identity overlap without relying on Christmas.

- Fit to the exact live provider template before production.

## Recommended first PRD concepts

1. **Snowy Cabin Breakfast Room** — winter hygge without requiring Christmas.
2. **Candlelit Lake Cabin Supper** — year-round cabincore / couple-gift lane.
3. **Fireside Knitting Cabin** — activity-driven hygge with excellent textile detail.

## What to avoid

- bare minimalist rooms with huge blank white walls
- generic Scandinavian folk-art if it stops looking like Hearthside painterly realism
- giant featureless snow fields
- hunting / trophy-cabin imagery that changes the audience
- luxury-resort interiors with little intimacy
- relying on `hygge` alone in SEO

## Decision

**Build Hygge/Cabincore as a combined lane, not two separate collections.** Use the concrete high-intent search language of cabins, winter, lake houses, fireplaces, and cozy rooms while using hygge to guide the emotional art direction and secondary SEO.
