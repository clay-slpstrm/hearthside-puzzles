# Hearthside Puzzles — Evergreen Puzzle Market Research

**Research date:** September 24, 2026  
**Business goal modeled:** **$10,000/month in Etsy revenue**  
**Store:** Hearthside Puzzles  
**Brand promise:** *Cozy puzzles for slow evenings in.*  
**Fulfillment strategy:** Printify primary, Printful qualified backup / comparison supplier

## Executive recommendation

Hearthside should not compete as a generic “pretty puzzle” shop. The strongest evergreen opportunity is a recognizable catalog of **cozy, painterly places people want to inhabit**, built around durable buyer identities: readers, cat lovers, cabin/lake lovers, homebodies, gardeners, and puzzle-gift buyers.

The evergreen portfolio should be organized around five repeatable lanes:

1. **Bookish Rooms** — reading nooks, home libraries, bookshops, rainy-window rooms.
2. **Cabin Weekends** — lake cabins, rustic kitchens, reading lofts, mountain lodges.
3. **Cat at Home** — the Hearthside orange tabby recurring inside distinctive rooms.
4. **Garden Rooms** — conservatories, potting rooms, sunrooms, greenhouse corners.
5. **Slow Evening Rituals** — baking, game nights, tea tables, puzzles, crafts.

The first three-week sprint should favor **Bookish Rooms + Cabin Weekends**, because current Etsy public result pages show a useful contrast between very broad competition and narrower scene-specific opportunities.

## Ranked evergreen opportunity lanes

### 1. Bookish Rooms

**Why it matters:** The audience naturally overlaps with Hearthside’s target customer: people who enjoy books, candles, blankets, quiet nights, gifts, and screen-free activities.

**Best concept families**

- Hidden Bookshop After Closing

**Puzzle advantage:** shelves, book-spine groups, lamps, rugs, windows, plants, tables, frames, and textiles create natural piece differentiation.

**Search-language seeds:** `reading nook puzzle`, `book lover puzzle`, `cozy bookstore puzzle`, `library jigsaw puzzle`, `bookshop puzzle`, `1000 piece book puzzle`.

### 2. Cabin Weekends

**Why it matters:** Etsy currently shows 665+ results for `cabin jigsaw puzzles`, with visible winter, autumn, lake, and rustic-cabin listings. It is active, but less saturated than broad “cozy puzzle” terms.

**Best concept families**
- Lakeside Cabin at Dusk
- Forest Cabin Reading Loft
- Rainy Cabin Kitchen
- Mountain Lodge Game Night
- Summer Porch by the Lake

**Puzzle advantage:** wood grain, plaid, stone hearths, foliage, reflections, firewood, windows, books, and rustic objects create excellent puzzle texture.

### 3. Cat at Home

Cat puzzles are crowded at the top level, but the orange tabby is already a Hearthside signature. The defensible move is to make the cat a **recurring character inside distinctive rooms**, not the entire design concept.

### 4. Garden Rooms

Conservatories and indoor garden rooms fit cottagecore / slow-living aesthetics while staying rooted in Hearthside’s interior-world promise.

**Concept families:** Rainy Glass Conservatory, Potting Room in Spring, Greenhouse Tea Table, Sunroom Full of Plants, Herb Kitchen Window.

### 5. Slow Evening Rituals

This is the most expandable lane and directly expresses the brand promise.

**Concept families:** Game Night by the Fire, Sunday Baking Kitchen, Tea & Crossword Table, Puzzle Table by the Window, Rainy Craft Room, Candlelit Supper for Two.

# POD Product Selection

## Primary Printify product — use this for Hearthside launch listings

### Puzzle (110, 252, 520, 1014-piece) — District Photo

**Decision:** **Primary production SKU.**

**Why this wins on paper**
- Marked **Bestseller** in Printify’s catalog.
- District Photo provider score was **9.3** at the time of research.
- Current catalog price starts at **$8.39**; Printify Premium from **$6.27**.
- US shipping shown from **$5.99**.
- Average production time shown around **2.2 days**.
- 100% chipboard.
- Glossy laminated finish.
- Printed paper box with the artwork on top.
- Includes a reference print.
- Horizontal and vertical variants.
- Adult-friendly sizes include **520 pieces** and **1014 pieces**.

**Horizontal finished sizes**
- 520 pieces: **20.00 × 15.98 in** — essentially Hearthside’s 5:4 master-art ratio.
- 1014 pieces: **28.50 × 19.02 in** — approximately 3:2.

### Recommended Hearthside use

**Default launch variant:** **520-piece horizontal**.  
It matches the existing Hearthside horizontal 5:4 / “4:5 horizontal” art system almost perfectly, reduces production cost, gives a satisfying adult build, and lets the same master artwork transfer cleanly to the backup Printful SKU.

**Premium / harder variant:** **1014-piece horizontal**.  
For every design that is launched at 1014 pieces, create a dedicated **3:2 extended canvas** rather than simply cropping the 5:4 master. Use generative outpainting or deliberate recomposition so important objects are not lost.

**Important:** order physical samples before treating this as fully approved. Provider ratings and catalog specs are not substitutes for testing piece fit, print density, glare, box quality, and shipping damage.

## Best Printful product — backup / controlled comparison SKU

### Printful Jigsaw Puzzle — 520 pieces

**Decision:** **Best Printful fit for Hearthside today. Use as a backup and quality-comparison SKU, especially for US orders.**

**Current product facts**
- 520-piece option.
- **19.74 × 15.74 in** finished size — very close to 5:4.
- Pressed paper chipboard with adhesive, **0.06 in / 1.53 mm thick**.
- Digital printing.
- Semi-gloss finish.
- Current listed starting price: **$15.25**.
- US shipping starts at **$5.89**.
- Product is currently **US-only**.
- Product page shows **60 reviews**.

### Why not use Printful’s 1000-piece tin puzzle as the default?

Printful’s Jigsaw Puzzle with Tin is visually attractive and gift-ready, but its current product page shows a **3.5/5 rating from 10 reviews** and multiple recent reviews specifically complain that many interior pieces have highly similar shapes, can fit in incorrect positions, or feel thin. For a brand whose customer happiness depends on the puzzle-building experience, that is a meaningful risk.

**Recommendation:** do not make the Printful tin the default Hearthside SKU. Sample it only if we later want a premium gift-packaging experiment.

## Why not make the Printify tin puzzle the default?

Printify’s Jigsaw Puzzle with Tin has excellent presentation, but its own description states that non-edge pieces are **uniformly cut**. Since Printful’s similarly configured tin puzzle has customer complaints about repetitive piece shape / false fits, Hearthside should prioritize the District Photo puzzle experience first and treat the tin as an optional future gift SKU after physical testing.

### Pricing rules

- Do **not** build the store around permanent 40–50% fake discounts.
- Use the normal list prices above as real anchors.
- Run modest seasonal or launch promotions in the stated bands.
- If offering free shipping, recalculate contribution using the exact SKU’s current production + shipping cost.
- If an exact 1014-piece live production cost moves materially above Printify’s $30–$34 planning range, raise the retail price toward **$57.95–$59.95** rather than compressing margin.

## $10,000/month revenue model

This is a planning model, not a sales forecast.

At a blended average order value around **$42** (mix of 520 and 1014 piece sales):

- `$10,000 / $42 ≈ 238 orders/month`
- roughly **8 orders/day**

If the blended AOV reaches **$45**, the target becomes roughly **223 orders/month**, or **7–8 orders/day**.

The first business objective is therefore not “100 designs.” It is to build enough coherent inventory to discover multiple listings capable of steady daily sales.

## Catalog roadmap

### Phase 1 — Proof of market: 0–30 designs
- Publish six new designs per week for five weeks.
- 3 evergreen + 3 seasonal during the 2026 holiday build.
- Track impressions, CTR, favorites, carts, orders, conversion, and ad efficiency.

### Phase 2 — Collection depth: 30–60 designs
- Make 2–3 sibling scenes for winning concepts.
- Build collection navigation around Bookish Rooms, Cabin Weekends, Seasonal Hearths, and Garden Rooms.

### Phase 3 — Scale: 60–100 active designs only if data supports it
- Expand winners rather than adding random scenes.
- Advertise only listings with evidence of demand.
- Add 1014-piece extended versions to proven designs first if production workflow becomes burdensome.

# Artwork Rules for Commercial Puzzle Performance

Every production design should satisfy:

- one clear focal area visible at Etsy thumbnail size
- layered foreground / midground / background
- **8–12 identifiable detail clusters**
- **3–5 repeated texture families**: books, brick, plaid, leaves, tile, wood grain, rain/snow, ceramics
- no giant flat dark wall
- no featureless sky, lake, rug, sofa, or snow field
- no illegible generated typography
- no text embedded in puzzle art unless added deliberately after generation
- warm/cool value separation so pieces remain distinguishable
- detail across the edges, but no critical face or focal object in trim-risk zones

## Master-art workflow

1. Generate the **5:4 horizontal master** at approximately **5000 × 4000 px or larger**.
2. Use it directly for:
   - Printify District Photo 520 horizontal
   - Printful 520 horizontal
3. Create a separate **3:2 extended master** for Printify 1014 horizontal:
   - approximately 6000 × 4000 px or larger
   - extend the scene outward instead of cropping focal content
4. Fit to the current live provider template before export.
