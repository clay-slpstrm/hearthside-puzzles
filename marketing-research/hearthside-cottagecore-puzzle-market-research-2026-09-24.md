# Hearthside Puzzles — Cottagecore Puzzle Market Research

**Research date:** September 24, 2026  
**Business target:** **$10,000/month Etsy revenue**  
**Channel:** Etsy  
**Fulfillment:** Printify / Printful POD  
**Brand:** Hearthside Puzzles — *Cozy puzzles for slow evenings in.*


## Executive recommendation

**Cottagecore is commercially attractive but too broad to attack as a generic aesthetic.** Etsy currently shows **4,000+ items** for `cottagecore puzzle`, with visible products spanning wildflowers, fantasy villages, animals, quilts, cottage gardens, bookshops, tea parties, woodland scenes, and branded/fandom-adjacent imagery.

For Hearthside, the opportunity is **Domestic Cottagecore**: cottage kitchens, potting rooms, sunrooms, sewing rooms, garden doors, tea tables, flowers, baskets, textiles, and rainy windows. This keeps the aesthetic grounded in Hearthside's strength—spaces people want to inhabit—rather than competing with generic floral fields or whimsical animal villages.

## Etsy market signal

Public Etsy result counts are directional competition proxies.

- `cottagecore puzzle`: **4,000+ items** — large but saturated.
- Visible physical examples include an **Autumn Cottagecore quilted cottage puzzle at $37.99**, a **Cottage Garden Frog 1000-piece puzzle at $34.99**, a **cozy quilt sewing shop puzzle with tin at $44.24**, a **wildflower meadow puzzle at $41.25**, and a **cozy bookshop cat puzzle at $33.75 sale price**.
- Multiple visible listings use 520/1014-piece Printify-style variants, proving that buyers already see this aesthetic on POD puzzle formats.
- The broad category is crowded with fantasy, kawaii, floral-only, and animal content. Hearthside should narrow into believable rooms and craft/garden activities.

## Customer profile

**Primary buyer**
- plant lover, gardener, baker, crafter, reader, cottage-home enthusiast
- likes natural materials, vintage ceramics, quilts, baskets, flowers, herbs, wood, and soft color
- seeks relaxing, feminine-neutral, giftable home imagery

**Emotional job**
> “Give me a quiet country room full of useful, beautiful little things.”

## Best sub-niches for Hearthside

### 1. Cottage Kitchen
Bread, herbs, checked linens, open shelves, painted cabinets, rain or garden view. Strongest evergreen domestic concept.

### 2. Potting Room / Garden Room
Terracotta, seed trays, gardening tools, shelves, gloves, watering cans, botanical variation. Excellent puzzle detail.

### 3. Quilting / Sewing Room
Fabric stacks, patchwork, thread, wooden table, baskets, lamp, window. Distinctive and underused relative to generic flower fields.

### 4. Garden-Door Sunroom
Indoor/outdoor transition with climbing flowers, tiles, wicker, tea, plants, and an open door. Good spring/summer extension.

## Recommended first PRD concepts

1. **Garden Cottage Kitchen** — strongest combination of baking, garden, and domestic slow-living cues.
2. **Rainy Potting Room** — highly puzzle-friendly, visually distinctive, and season-flexible.
3. **Quilter's Sunroom** — craft identity + textiles + light, with less direct overlap with generic flower puzzles.

## What to avoid

- giant wildflower fields with little piece differentiation
- kawaii rabbits / gnomes / anthropomorphic villages as the default style
- candy-pastel palettes that weaken Hearthside's richer tone
- fantasy mushrooms or fairy houses unless deliberately testing a brand extension
- generic floral collages with no story or room
- hyper-clean modern farmhouse styling

## Decision

**Build Cottagecore, but narrow it aggressively.** The broad niche is too saturated to be the product concept by itself. Hearthside should own **domestic cottagecore rooms and activities** where the brand's warm-light, storybook-realism system gives us a clearer point of difference.
