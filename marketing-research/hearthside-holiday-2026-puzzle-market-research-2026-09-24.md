# Hearthside Puzzles — 2026 Holiday Season Puzzle Market Research

**Research date:** September 24, 2026  
**Selling window:** October–December 2026, with winter carryover into Q1 2027  
**Business goal modeled:** **$10,000/month in Etsy revenue**  
**Core collection:** Seasonal Hearths  
**Fulfillment strategy:** Printify primary, Printful backup / comparison

## Executive recommendation

Holiday 2026 should be the fastest catalog-building period for Hearthside, but the store should still look like Hearthside in January. The strongest seasonal strategy is to combine Christmas gift intent with the brand’s durable cozy worlds:

1. **Christmas Rooms** — decorated living rooms, kitchens, libraries, window seats.
2. **Snowy Cabins** — libraries, kitchens, game rooms, porch scenes, lodge interiors.
3. **Holiday Bookish** — Christmas bookshops, reading rooms, wrapped-book evenings.
4. **Winter Village Windows** — bookshop, bakery, café, and general-store corners.
5. **Holiday Cats at Home** — orange tabby integrated into believable rooms.

**Creative rule:** sell the feeling of **coming inside from the cold**, not a pile of Christmas symbols.

## Ranked Holiday 2026 opportunity lanes

### 1. Christmas Rooms

The purest translation of Hearthside’s existing Seasonal Hearths collection.

**Best scenes**
- Christmas Eve Reading Nook
- Quiet Christmas Morning Kitchen
- Holiday Game Night by the Fire
- Candlelit Holiday Supper
- Snowy Window Seat with Wrapped Books
- Holiday Library Hearth

**Visual cues:** modest tree, stockings, garland, paper stars, wrapped gifts, dried oranges, candles, wool/plaid textiles, warm lamps, snow outside.

**Avoid:** oversized Santa imagery, cartoon elves, candy palettes, giant text signs.

### 2. Snowy Cabins

Cabin puzzles already show meaningful activity, and winter makes them highly giftable while allowing post-Christmas carryover.

**Best scenes:** Snowed-In Cabin Library, Mountain Lodge Puzzle Night, Cabin Kitchen with Cinnamon Rolls, Winter Porch Lanterns, Lakeside Cabin Under Snow.

### 3. Holiday Bookish

Bookish Christmas scenes create a strong three-way intersection: **puzzler + reader + holiday gift**.

**Best scenes:** Christmas Bookshop After Closing, Holiday Library Tree, Snowy Book Café, Christmas Attic Reading Nook, Bookstore Window on Christmas Eve.

### 4. Winter Village Windows

Excellent for thumbnail appeal and puzzle texture: brick, windows, snow, garland, cobblestones, displays, bicycles, streetlamps, and interior glimpses.

### 5. Holiday Cats at Home

Use the orange tabby as a recurring emotional character inside stronger scene concepts: under the tree, on the snowy window seat, in the kitchen, beside the library hearth.

## Tin-box decision for Holiday 2026

A tin looks excellent for gifting, but Hearthside should not trade puzzle satisfaction for packaging.

- Printify’s tin puzzle explicitly states **uniformly cut non-edge pieces**.
- Printful’s similar tin puzzle currently has **3.5/5 from 10 reviews**, with multiple reviews describing repetitive piece shapes / incorrect fits.

**Decision:** do not use the tin product as the Holiday 2026 default. Order one sample later as an experiment, but launch the store on the District Photo paper-box product unless physical testing reverses this decision.

# Holiday Pricing Architecture

Use the same normal MSRP across evergreen and seasonal listings. Avoid artificial holiday markups that make January pricing awkward.

| Variant | Normal Hearthside price | Holiday promo band | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **520-piece** | **$36.95** | **$32.95–$34.95** | Core gift / family size |
| **1014-piece** | **$54.95** | **$49.95–$52.95** | Premium adult challenge |

### Pricing guardrail

Printify’s published planning model estimates average costs around **$15–$17 for ~500-piece puzzles** and **$30–$34 for ~1000-piece puzzles**, with suggested retail around **$30–$40** and **$50–$60** respectively. Exact District Photo variant costs must be checked live.

If free shipping is offered, calculate the actual contribution after production, shipping, Etsy fees, ad costs, and discounts. If the 1014-piece live cost increases, move the normal price toward **$57.95–$59.95** rather than relying on very thin holiday margins.

# Holiday Artwork Rules

Every seasonal scene should retain Hearthside’s signature:

- cozy painterly realism
- one dominant warm light source
- gentle shadows
- amber / rust / forest / muted blue / cream palette
- restrained cranberry or evergreen accents rather than candy red/green
- foreground / midground / background layers
- 8+ distinct object clusters
- 3+ texture families
- exterior weather detail without blank snowfields
- no random pseudo-signage
- no generic Santa / gnome / elf clip-art composition

## Master-art workflow

**520-piece:** 5:4 horizontal master, approximately 5000 × 4000 px or larger.  
**1014-piece:** create a dedicated 3:2 extended master, approximately 6000 × 4000 px or larger.

Do not simply crop the Christmas tree, cat, hearth, or other focal element to force the 1014 format.
