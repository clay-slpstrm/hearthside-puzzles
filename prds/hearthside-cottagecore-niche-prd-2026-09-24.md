# Hearthside Puzzles — Cottagecore Niche PRD

**PRD date:** September 24, 2026  
**Niche:** Domestic Cottagecore  
**Production goal:** 3 new finished puzzle designs / 3 Etsy listings  
**Business target:** $10,000/month Etsy revenue

## Commercial brief

Broad `cottagecore puzzle` competition is high, so Hearthside will not compete with generic wildflower fields, fantasy villages, or cute animal scenes. This PRD targets **domestic cottagecore**—rooms and practical hobbies that buyers can imagine inhabiting: cooking, gardening, quilting, tea, rain, plants, and handmade textiles.

**Primary SEO lane:** scene term first, `cottagecore` second.  
**Primary conversion hook:** “I want that quiet country room.”

# Garden Cottage Kitchen

**Collection:** Cozy Rooms — Domestic Cottagecore  
**Primary buyer:** bakers, gardeners, cottagecore buyers, homebodies  
**Search-language seed:** `cottage kitchen puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** Fresh bread, herbs from the garden, and nowhere to be for the rest of the afternoon.

## Product Requirement

A lived-in country cottage kitchen with painted cabinets, open shelves, a worktable, herbs and flowers from the garden, fresh bread, and a view through an open or rain-specked door. The room is cozy and useful, not styled like a showroom.

## Must-Include Puzzle Details

- painted cabinets and open wood shelves
- wood worktable with fresh bread, apples, mixing bowl, tea, checked cloth
- herb pots, cut flowers, basket of garden produce
- copper pan and ceramic crocks
- garden door or window with climbing flowers
- orange tabby on a chair or floor
- patterned tile, woven basket, wooden utensils

## Lighting / Palette

Dominant light: warm pendant over the worktable. Secondary: soft garden daylight. Palette: cream, sage, warm amber, terracotta, dusty blue, rust, natural wood.

## ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 Hearthside jigsaw puzzle of a lived-in garden cottage kitchen on a gentle afternoon. Center a wooden worktable beneath one warm pendant lamp. On the table: fresh bread, apples, ceramic mixing bowl, teapot, checked linen, wooden utensils, and a small bunch of garden herbs. Surround the scene with painted cream-and-sage cabinets, open shelves of mismatched ceramics, copper pan, crocks, terracotta herb pots, cut flowers, woven produce basket, patterned tile, and an orange tabby resting near a chair. Through a garden door or large window, show climbing roses, herbs, and soft greenery with enough structure to avoid a flat green area. Cozy painterly realism, adult storybook book-cover quality, visible brush texture, believable country home, rich puzzle detail. No people, no text, no farmhouse signs, no candy-pastel styling, no hyper-clean showroom.

## Quality-Control Notes

- Avoid generic “farmhouse” typography.
