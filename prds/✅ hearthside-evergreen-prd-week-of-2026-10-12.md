## Production Product Decision

**Product:** Puzzle (110, 252, 520, 1014-piece)  
**Provider:** District Photo  
**Default launch variant:** **520 pcs, Horizontal — 20.00 × 15.98 in**  
**Premium variant:** **1014 pcs, Horizontal — 28.50 × 19.02 in**  
**Material / finish:** 100% chipboard, glossy laminated finish  
**Packaging:** paper box with artwork printed on top; reference print included  
**Current catalog signals:** Bestseller; provider score about 9.3; from $8.39; US shipping from $5.99; avg. production time about 2.2 days at research time.

**Why selected:** the 520-piece horizontal format maps almost perfectly to Hearthside's existing 5:4 horizontal art system, while District Photo offers a 1014-piece adult challenge in the same product family. The printed box is giftable without accepting the “uniform non-edge piece” warning attached to the Printify tin product.

### Backup / comparison SKU — Printful

**Product:** Jigsaw Puzzle  
**Variant:** **520 pieces — 19.74 × 15.74 in**  
**Material:** 0.06 in / 1.53 mm pressed paper chipboard  
**Print / finish:** digital print, semi-gloss  
**Current price:** from $15.25  
**US shipping:** from $5.89  
**Availability:** US only

**Use:** backup supplier and controlled sample comparison. The same 5:4 master artwork should transfer with only template-level adjustment.

### Do not use as the default

Do **not** default Hearthside to the Printful 1000-piece tin or the Printify tin product. The Printify tin explicitly uses uniformly cut non-edge pieces, while Printful's similar tin product currently has multiple reviews complaining about repetitive piece shapes / false fits. Customer puzzle experience takes priority over metal packaging.

## Technical Artwork Deliverables

**520-piece / Printful 520 master**
- horizontal **5:4** composition
- generate at **5000 × 4000 px or larger**
- preserve 5–7% safe breathing room around critical focal objects
- fit final art to the current live provider template

**1014-piece premium variant**
- horizontal **3:2** extended composition
- generate / outpaint at **6000 × 4000 px or larger**
- extend scene left/right; do not simply crop the 5:4 master
- preserve the same focal point and storytelling hierarchy

**Shared Hearthside puzzle rules**
- cozy painterly realism; adult illustrated-book-cover feel
- one dominant warm light source, with restrained cool ambient contrast
- foreground / midground / background layers
- 8–12 identifiable detail clusters
- 3–5 strong texture families
- no giant flat dark walls, skies, floors, water, or snow fields
- no text inside the generated artwork
- no neon, hyper-plastic 3D, or fantasy drift unless explicitly requested
- recurring orange tabby may appear, but should not overwhelm the scene

# Design 1 — Library Hearth & Orange Tabby

**Collection:** Cozy Rooms  
**Primary buyer:** readers, cat lovers, traditional-home lovers  
**Search-language seed:** `library jigsaw puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** A library that looks like it has been waiting all day for evening.

## Product Requirement

A traditional home library at night with modest stone hearth, built-in shelves, two reading chairs, patterned rug, and an orange tabby stretched near the fire. The scene should feel lived-in rather than staged.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- stone/brick hearth with distinct shapes
- built-in shelves with lower cabinets
- two different upholstered chairs
- patterned rug with border motifs
- orange tabby stretched naturally
- tea, glasses, books, plant, firewood basket, low lamp

### Lighting / Palette

Fire as dominant glow, low shaded lamp secondary. Palette: deep brown, amber, forest green, muted blue, cream, rust.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 Hearthside jigsaw puzzle of a traditional home library on a quiet evening. Center a modest stone hearth with glowing fire as the dominant warm light. Built-in wooden bookshelves fill the side walls with varied book clusters and small decor. Place two different upholstered reading chairs at angles around a low table, with a rust-and-forest patterned rug beneath them and an orange tabby stretched across one edge of the rug. Add tea, glasses, stacked books, small plant, firewood basket, framed art without text, and a low shaded lamp as secondary light. Cozy painterly realism, adult illustrated-book-cover quality, visible soft brushwork, rich mid-sized puzzle details with clear hierarchy. No people, no readable titles, no giant black shadows, no mansion-scale fantasy.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

traditional cozy home library evening, modest stone fireplace, built-in bookshelves, two upholstered chairs, patterned rust forest rug, orange tabby, tea glasses books plant firewood basket low shaded lamp, amber forest muted blue cream palette, painterly realism adult storybook --ar 5:4 --stylize 175 --no readable words mansion fantasy neon huge shadows 3d

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Correct shelves, chair legs, rug perspective, fireplace stones, cat anatomy, and table objects. Separate shelf sections into distinct color/value groups and make the rug border crisp enough to guide puzzling. Keep fire primary and retain shadow detail. Remove book-title text.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 with additional shelf bays / cabinet detail at left and a window / plant / small reading table zone at right. Keep fireplace and chairs centered and avoid empty wall extensions.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Avoid overly symmetrical AI-showroom composition.
- Bookshelves need variety but believable organization.
- Firelight should not turn entire image orange.
- Rug pattern must read both large and small.

**1014 extension filename:** `hearthside_cozy-rooms_library-hearth-orange-tabby_1014_3x2.png`

# Design 2 — Mountain Lodge Game Night

**Collection:** Cabin Weekends  
**Primary buyer:** families, couples, game-night and cabin gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `mountain lodge puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** The weather can do whatever it wants; the game is already on the table.

## Product Requirement

A mountain lodge living room during a rainy windstorm. A round table holds an original wordless game, cocoa, and snacks; a pendant lamp and stone hearth warm the room while windows show wind-blown pines.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- round game table with generic unbranded pieces/cards
- stone hearth and firewood
- pendant lamp over table
- stormy divided window with pine branches
- plaid blankets, coat hooks, boots
- mugs, snack bowl, blank score pad, bookshelf

### Lighting / Palette

Pendant lamp over table is dominant; hearth secondary; cool storm blue through windows. Palette: amber, forest, rust, cream, muted storm blue.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of a mountain lodge game night during a rainy windstorm. A round wooden table is the focal point beneath one warm pendant lamp, set with an original unbranded tabletop game: simple illustrated cards with no words, wooden tokens, dice-like cubes, cocoa mugs, and a snack bowl. Nearby, a stone fireplace adds warm secondary glow. Through divided windows, rain streaks and wind-blown pine branches create cool contrast. Include plaid blankets, hanging coats, boots by the door, a small bookshelf, firewood basket, patterned cushions, and wood beams. No people; imply players just stepped away. Adult storybook realism, visible brush texture, many clear detail clusters. No logos, no branded board game, no readable text.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Correct table ellipse, card perspective, token shapes, mugs, fireplace, windows, and furniture. Keep game components generic and text-free. Strengthen plaid, stone, wood grain, coats, and pine textures into separate puzzle regions. Maintain pendant as focal light.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 with a larger coat/boot/wood-storage zone on one side and additional storm-window / shelving detail on the other. Do not shrink the game table.

### Quality-Control Notes

- No recognizable copyrighted game.
- Avoid tiny unreadable game clutter.
- Storm should remain cozy, not threatening.
- Table must read instantly at thumbnail size.

# Design 3 — Puzzle Table by the Window

**Collection:** Cozy Rooms  
**Primary buyer:** puzzlers, couples, retirement and hobby gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `puzzle lover jigsaw puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** A puzzle about the exact kind of evening the customer wants to have.

## Product Requirement

A warm den with an in-progress generic jigsaw on a wooden table beside a twilight window. Sorting trays, lamp, tea, books, blanket, orange tabby, shelves, and rug create a meta-puzzling scene.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- large wooden puzzle table with generic landscape pieces
- sorting trays grouped by color
- warm shaded table lamp
- twilight tree view
- orange tabby on nearby chair
- mug, glasses, books, blanket, shelf, plant, patterned rug

### Lighting / Palette

Shaded table lamp as dominant warm source; cool twilight at window. Palette: amber, rust, forest, muted blue, cream.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle scene built around an evening puzzle table beside a window. A large wooden table holds an in-progress generic jigsaw image made of simple landscape colors with no recognizable copyrighted art, plus several sorting trays of pieces. A shaded table lamp is the single strong warm light. Nearby are a ceramic mug, reading glasses, two books, folded blanket, small plant, and an orange tabby resting on an upholstered chair. Through the window, muted twilight trees and the last blue light of evening. Add shelves, wood grain, patterned rug, fabric folds, and subtle decor for puzzle-friendly detail. Adult illustrated-book-cover feeling, calm, warm, believable, screen-free. No people, no text, no logos, no giant blank wall, no recursive hyper-detailed inner puzzle image.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Keep the inner jigsaw generic and simple. Correct piece shapes, table perspective, trays, chair, cat, lamp, and window. Make sorting trays distinct by color families and strengthen rug, wood, books, and fabric as puzzle cues. Remove accidental text or branded objects.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 by widening the den with an additional shelf/cabinet area and more window/curtain detail. Preserve the table-lamp focal area and keep the inner jigsaw visually simple.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Inner puzzle must not become confusing with the actual product.
- Keep piece shapes plausible, not cloned.
- No trademarked puzzle boxes or brands.
- Lamp/table should remain obvious focal zone.
