# Rainy Book Café Window

**Collection:** Cozy Rooms / Quiet Shops — Bookish  
**Primary buyer:** readers, café lovers, book-club gifts  
**Search-language seed:** `book cafe puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** A corner table, a new book, and rain making the rest of the city feel far away.

## Product Requirement

A small independent book café viewed from inside on a rainy afternoon. One corner table beside the front window holds books, tea/coffee, pastry, glasses, and a lamp; shelves and café details fill the background. No people required.

## Must-Include Puzzle Details

- corner table with stacked books, open book, mug, pastry, glasses
- rain-streaked front window with wet-street reflections
- small shaded table lamp as warm focal light
- wood bookshelves integrated into café
- counter with pastry dome, kettle, ceramics
- chairs, coat hook, plant, patterned tile or rug
- subtle orange tabby on a window bench or shelf

## Lighting / Palette

Dominant light: small shaded lamp at the reading table. Secondary: warm café pendants + cool rainy window. Palette: amber, mocha, forest, muted teal/blue, cream, rust.

## ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 Hearthside jigsaw puzzle of a small independent book café on a rainy afternoon. Focus on a cozy corner table beside a tall rain-streaked window. On the table: an open book with no readable text, two stacked books, ceramic mug, small pastry plate, reading glasses, and a shaded table lamp that provides the dominant warm glow. In the background blend a real café with bookshelves: wood shelves, counter with pastry dome and kettle, mismatched cups, a few chairs, coat hooks, plants, and patterned tile or rug. Wet street reflections and umbrellas outside add cool blue contrast. Include a subtle orange tabby on a window bench or lower shelf. Adult painterly realism, book-cover quality, rich mid-sized detail, believable small-business interior. No people as focal subjects, no readable menus or shop signs, no brand logos, no fantasy magic, no neon.

## Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

## Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Refine the book café for premium puzzle production. Correct table, chairs, cup handles, pastry dome, kettle, shelves, windows, and cat. Remove all menu/shop/book text. Break shelves and café objects into distinct color/value clusters. Keep the small reading-table lamp as the focal warm source while allowing softer café lighting in the background.

## 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend the 3:2 version with more storefront/window and one additional shelf/counter bay. Keep the corner reading table as the clear focal point.

## Quality-Control Notes

- No readable café menu or storefront text.
- The space must read as both bookstore and café without clutter.
- Food should remain secondary to the reading experience.
- Keep exterior reflections detailed, not a blue blur.

# Upstairs Used Bookshop

**Collection:** Quiet Shops — Bookish  
**Primary buyer:** bookstore lovers, collectors, bookworm gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `used bookstore puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** The second floor is where the good books are hiding.

## Product Requirement

A narrow upstairs floor of a used bookstore with sloped ceilings, crowded-but-organized shelves, creaky wood floor, reading chair, rolling cart, boxes, lamp, plants, and a small window. Distinct from the earlier storefront-after-closing design.

## Must-Include Puzzle Details

- sloped ceiling or old plaster beams
- shelves at different heights with clear book clusters
- rolling book cart
- worn reading chair and small side lamp
- cardboard archive boxes / wooden crates with no text
- small dormer or sash window
- plant, rug runner, mug, stool, orange tabby on a shelf or crate

## Lighting / Palette

Dominant light: shaded floor or side-table lamp. Secondary: soft cloudy daylight through a small upstairs window. Palette: honeyed wood, olive, rust, muted navy, cream, faded burgundy.

## ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of the upstairs floor of a small used bookstore. The old room has a sloped ceiling, creaky wood floor, shelves of varied heights packed with visually distinct book groups, a rolling book cart, worn reading chair, small side table, and a shaded lamp as the dominant warm light. Add wooden crates and archive boxes with no readable text, a small sash or dormer window, runner rug, plant, mug, stool, and an orange tabby perched naturally on a low crate or shelf. The shop should feel slightly eccentric but believable and organized enough to navigate. Adult illustrated-book-cover quality, visible brush texture, rich book/wood/fabric/paper details for a 1000-piece-friendly image. No people, no readable titles, no fantasy elements, no giant blank ceiling.

## Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Polish the used-bookshop room. Correct shelf perspective, sloped ceiling geometry, cart wheels, chair legs, crates, window, and cat. Remove all book/box text. Separate books into intentional color/value groups and preserve readable floor/rug texture in shadow.

Extend laterally with an additional short shelf and another crate/window-wall zone. Do not turn the attic-like room into a large commercial floor.

## Quality-Control Notes

- Books should not form one continuous brown texture.
- No bookstore logos or generated signage.

# Sunday Home Library Table

**Collection:** Cozy Rooms — Bookish  
**Primary buyer:** readers, couples, home-library aspirational buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `home library puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** A long Sunday afternoon with books open on the table and no plans afterward.

## Product Requirement

A comfortable home library centered on a large communal reading table rather than a fireplace. Shelves, shaded lamps, tea, books, flowers, notes, and windows create a bright, sophisticated book-lover scene.

## Must-Include Puzzle Details

- large wood reading table with 2–3 reading stations
- shaded table lamps
- open books and stacked books without text
- tea pot, mugs, glasses, vase with simple flowers
- built-in shelves with art and plants
- window with trees or soft rain
- patterned rug, upholstered bench or chairs, orange tabby under table

## Lighting / Palette

Dominant light: two warm shaded table lamps functioning as one focal zone. Secondary: muted afternoon window light. Palette: cream, walnut, forest green, muted blue, rust, brass.

## ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of an elegant but lived-in home library on a slow Sunday afternoon. Center a large wooden communal reading table with two warm shaded table lamps forming one clear focal zone. Arrange several open and stacked books with no readable text, a teapot, two mugs, reading glasses, simple flower vase, bookmark ribbons, and small note cards with no lettering. Built-in bookshelves line the room with distinct book-color clusters, framed art without text, plants, lower cabinets, and comfortable chairs. Through a window show leafy trees or soft rain. Add a patterned rug and a subtle orange tabby sleeping beneath the table. Adult illustrated-book-cover style, cozy painterly realism, sophisticated and attainable, rich textures across wood, paper, fabric, ceramics, foliage, and books. No people, no readable titles, no fantasy library, no mansion scale.

## Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

## Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

## 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend the library with partial additional shelf bays / window space on each side while preserving the table as the central composition.

## Quality-Control Notes

- Avoid formal mansion grandeur.
- Tabletop objects should form 3–4 clear clusters, not clutter.
- No readable book titles or framed text.
- Keep the room bright enough for puzzle-piece differentiation.
