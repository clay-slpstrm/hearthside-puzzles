This is the most time-sensitive new lane. Build 3–6 differentiated winter-village puzzles now, using Hearthside warm-window realism rather than flat folk-art imitation. Keep two designs generic-winter enough to sell through January.

## Buyer and brand fit

This niche fits Hearthside when the scene remains believable, warm, tactile and inhabitable. The finished puzzle should feel like adult illustrated-book-cover art: one clear warm light source, rich but gentle color, layered foreground/midground/background, and enough mid-sized detail to make 520–1014 pieces satisfying.

## White-space opportunities

- bookshop/bakery village streets with detailed windows
- snowy harbor or canal towns at blue hour
- interior-through-window viewpoints that preserve Hearthside intimacy
- post-Christmas winter variants without Santa/text

## Research method and limitations

This research combines current public Etsy market/result pages, current public eRank trend reporting, current EverBee research guidance, and the Hearthside brand/product constraints. Etsy result counts are **directional competition proxies**, not exact market size or monthly demand. Visible review counts and prices show that products have sold, but do not reveal exact current velocity.

I could not directly invoke the user's locally installed eRank or EverBee Chrome extensions from this ChatGPT session. Their authenticated extension-only search volume, estimated sales, revenue, and conversion metrics are therefore **not claimed** here. Public eRank and EverBee pages were used where available. A follow-up pass can incorporate extension data if the user exports CSVs or uploads screenshots from the extension overlays.
