# Snowy Cabin Breakfast Room

**Collection:** Cabin Weekends — Hygge / Cabincore  
**Primary buyer:** winter-cozy buyers, cabin lovers, couples, gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `snowy cabin puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** The storm can keep going; breakfast is warm and the fire is already lit.

## Product Requirement

A small timber cabin breakfast room after overnight snow. A wood table with bread, jam, coffee, wool textiles, and simple ceramics sits near a stove/fireplace and snow-heavy windows. Winter rather than Christmas.

## Must-Include Puzzle Details

- wood breakfast table with bread, jam, butter dish, coffee/tea
- simple ceramics and woven placemats
- wood stove or modest stone fireplace
- wool blanket and knit textures
- snow-heavy divided windows with close pine branches
- boots, firewood basket, wall hooks, small shelf of books
- optional orange tabby on bench

## Lighting / Palette

Dominant light: warm pendant or stove/fire glow around the table. Secondary: bright blue-white winter morning through windows. Palette: honey wood, cream, charcoal, forest green, rust, muted blue.

## ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of a small timber cabin breakfast room after an overnight snowfall. Center a wooden breakfast table with fresh bread, jam jar with no readable label, butter dish, ceramic coffee/tea pot, two mugs, woven placemats, and simple dishes. A modest wood stove or stone fireplace and one warm pendant create the dominant cozy light. Add a wool blanket on a bench, knit texture, boots, firewood basket, coat hooks, small bookshelf, and an optional orange tabby curled on the bench. Through divided windows show close pine branches heavy with snow, layered blue-white shadows, and enough branch detail to avoid blank snow fields. Adult storybook painterly realism, intimate hygge feeling, rich wood/wool/ceramic/snow texture. No Christmas tree, no text, no luxury lodge, no minimalist white void.

## Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

snowy cabin breakfast room, timber interior, wood table bread jam butter coffee mugs woven placemats, modest stove fireplace warm pendant, wool blanket boots firewood hooks bookshelf optional orange tabby, divided windows snow-heavy pine branches, hygge cabincore painterly realism adult jigsaw --ar 5:4 --stylize 180 --no Christmas tree words luxury lodge minimalist void neon

## Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Refine the cabin breakfast scene. Correct table, dishes, bread, fireplace/stove, windows, boots, and cat. Remove label text. Strengthen grain, knit, woven mats, ceramics, bark, pine needles, and snow clumps as separate puzzle textures. Preserve cool morning window light against warm interior glow.

## 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend the room sideways with more window/bench detail and a small shelf/coat-hook zone. Do not enlarge the snow view into an empty field.

## Quality-Control Notes

- Must sell as winter beyond Christmas.
- Snow should have branches, frame lines, and shadows for puzzle differentiation.
- Keep the cabin modest and attainable.
- Breakfast food must look natural, not glossy plastic.

# Candlelit Lake Cabin Supper

**Collection:** Cabin Weekends — Hygge / Cabincore  
**Primary buyer:** couples, lake-house buyers, anniversary / housewarming gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `lake cabin puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** A simple supper by the lake after the last light leaves the water.

## Product Requirement

A lake-cabin dining nook at dusk with a small round table, supper for two, candles, window water view, wood walls, books, wool, and a quiet dock outside. No people needed; presence is implied.

## Must-Include Puzzle Details

- round table with two place settings, bread, soup or simple supper
- two candles as main warm source plus subtle wall lamp
- wide divided window with lake, dock, canoe, pine shoreline
- wool throw and cushioned bench
- books, flowers/greenery, ceramic pitcher, woven basket
- wood paneling / beams, patterned rug
- boots or jacket near door

## Lighting / Palette

Dominant light: paired candles on the table functioning as one focal source; subtle wall sconce secondary. Cool blue lake dusk outside. Palette: amber, deep pine, muted lake blue, rust, cream, dark wood.

A cushioned bench with a wool throw sits beneath a wide divided window overlooking a quiet lake, small dock, tied canoe, and pine shoreline in muted blue evening light. Add books, a simple vase of greenery, woven basket, boots or jacket near the door, wood paneling, ceiling beams, and patterned rug. No people; imply they just stepped away. Adult illustrated-book-cover quality, hygge / cabincore warmth, visible soft brushwork, detailed but calm. No text, no luxury-resort scale, no giant empty lake, no romantic clichés like rose petals.

# Fireside Knitting Cabin

**Collection:** Cabin Weekends — Hygge / Cabincore  
**Primary buyer:** knitters, crafters, homebodies, winter gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `cozy winter puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** A chair by the fire, a basket of wool, and one more row before bed.

## Product Requirement

A rustic cabin craft corner with armchair, knitting basket, partially knitted blanket/scarf, fire, lamp, books, tea, snow/rain window, and layered textiles. Craft identity without showing a person.

## Must-Include Puzzle Details

- stone or brick hearth
- comfortable armchair with knit throw
- basket of yarn balls in restrained colors
- needles and partially knitted textile positioned plausibly
- side table with tea and books
- window with rain or snow and close branches
- rug, firewood, slippers, shelf, orange tabby near hearth

## Lighting / Palette

Dominant light: hearth fire with one low shaded reading lamp as controlled secondary. Palette: amber, oatmeal, forest, rust, muted blue, charcoal, natural wool tones.

## ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of a fireside knitting corner in a rustic cabin on a cold evening. A comfortable upholstered armchair sits near a modest stone or brick hearth. Beside it, a woven basket holds yarn balls in oatmeal, forest green, rust, muted blue, and cream, with knitting needles and a partially knitted scarf or blanket arranged plausibly. Add a low side table with tea and stacked books, a shaded reading lamp, patterned rug, firewood, slippers, small shelf, and an orange tabby resting near the hearth. Through a divided window show rain or snow against close branches. The fire is the dominant warm source; the lamp is secondary. Adult illustrated-book-cover painterly realism, tactile wool/stone/wood/fabric detail for a satisfying jigsaw. No people, no text, no oversized yarn, no cartoon craft aesthetic, no blank dark walls.

## Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

## Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Refine the knitting cabin. Correct chair, hearth stones, yarn strands, needles, basket weaving, table, lamp, cat, and window. Make the knitted textile pattern clear but not microscopic. Preserve shadow detail around the hearth and remove any text on books.

Extend sideways with more bookshelf/window detail and a small wood-storage zone, keeping chair/hearth/yarn as the focal triangle.

## Quality-Control Notes

- Knitting needles and yarn must be physically plausible.
- Textile detail should help puzzling, not become visual noise.
- Firelight should stay warm without flattening the room orange.
- Avoid decorative antlers / hunting-trophy styling.
