## Weekly Commercial Brief

Week 2 leans into snowy cabins and bookish gifting. The art should feel holiday-ready but premium enough to frame, with dense, satisfying texture rather than large fields of snow.

## Production Product Decision

### Primary production SKU — Printify

**Product:** Puzzle (110, 252, 520, 1014-piece)  
**Provider:** District Photo  
**Default launch variant:** **520 pcs, Horizontal — 20.00 × 15.98 in**  
**Premium variant:** **1014 pcs, Horizontal — 28.50 × 19.02 in**  
**Material / finish:** 100% chipboard, glossy laminated finish  
**Packaging:** paper box with artwork printed on top; reference print included  
**Current catalog signals:** Bestseller; provider score about 9.3; from $8.39; US shipping from $5.99; avg. production time about 2.2 days at research time.

**Why selected:** the 520-piece horizontal format maps almost perfectly to Hearthside's existing 5:4 horizontal art system, while District Photo offers a 1014-piece adult challenge in the same product family. The printed box is giftable without accepting the “uniform non-edge piece” warning attached to the Printify tin product.

### Backup / comparison SKU — Printful

**Product:** Jigsaw Puzzle  
**Variant:** **520 pieces — 19.74 × 15.74 in**  
**Material:** 0.06 in / 1.53 mm pressed paper chipboard  
**Print / finish:** digital print, semi-gloss  
**Current price:** from $15.25  
**US shipping:** from $5.89  
**Availability:** US only

**Use:** backup supplier and controlled sample comparison. The same 5:4 master artwork should transfer with only template-level adjustment.

### Do not use as the default

Do **not** default Hearthside to the Printful 1000-piece tin or the Printify tin product. The Printify tin explicitly uses uniformly cut non-edge pieces, while Printful's similar tin product currently has multiple reviews complaining about repetitive piece shapes / false fits. Customer puzzle experience takes priority over metal packaging.

## Technical Artwork Deliverables

**520-piece / Printful 520 master**
- horizontal **5:4** composition
- generate at **5000 × 4000 px or larger**
- preserve 5–7% safe breathing room around critical focal objects
- fit final art to the current live provider template

**1014-piece premium variant**
- horizontal **3:2** extended composition
- generate / outpaint at **6000 × 4000 px or larger**
- extend scene left/right; do not simply crop the 5:4 master
- preserve the same focal point and storytelling hierarchy

**Shared Hearthside puzzle rules**
- cozy painterly realism; adult illustrated-book-cover feel
- one dominant warm light source, with restrained cool ambient contrast
- foreground / midground / background layers
- 8–12 identifiable detail clusters
- 3–5 strong texture families
- no giant flat dark walls, skies, floors, water, or snow fields
- no text inside the generated artwork
- no neon, hyper-plastic 3D, or fantasy drift unless explicitly requested
- recurring orange tabby may appear, but should not overwhelm the scene

# Design 1 — Snowed-In Cabin Library

**Collection:** Seasonal Hearths / Cabin Weekends  
**Primary buyer:** book lovers, cabin lovers, winter gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `snowy cabin puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** The road is buried, the fire is lit, and nobody is going anywhere.

## Product Requirement

A small cabin library after heavy snow, with pine shelves, stone hearth, deep chair, orange tabby, wool blanket, lantern, books, boots, and windows packed with snow-covered branches.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- pine bookshelves
- stone hearth
- deep reading chair
- orange tabby on footstool
- wool plaid blanket
- lantern and mug
- snow-heavy branch-filled window
- firewood, boots, basket

### Lighting / Palette

Fireplace dominates; lantern secondary; exterior blue-gray snow contrast. Palette: amber, pine green, rust, cream, winter blue.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 Hearthside jigsaw puzzle of a small snowed-in cabin library at night. Pine bookshelves line the walls around a stone fireplace, the main warm light source. A deep upholstered reading chair holds a wool plaid blanket; an orange tabby sleeps on the footstool. Add a lantern, mug, open book, firewood basket, snow boots, woven basket, small framed art with no text, and shelf objects. Through divided windows, heavy snow clings to close pine branches, filling the exterior with layered whites, blue-gray shadows, and dark green needles instead of a blank snow field. Cozy painterly realism, adult book-cover illustration, soft brush texture. No people, no readable text, no fantasy lodge, no giant blank snow.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

snowed-in cabin library, pine bookshelves, stone fireplace warm light, deep chair wool plaid blanket, orange tabby footstool, lantern mug open book firewood boots basket, windows packed snowy pine branches, cozy painterly realism --ar 5:4 --stylize 185 --no words huge blank snow fantasy mansion neon

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Correct bookshelves, fireplace stones, chair, footstool, cat, boots, windows, and branches. Add distinct snow clumps, pine needles, plaid blocks, shelf color groups, wood grain, and stone variation. Preserve shadow detail and remove pseudo-text.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 with one additional bookshelf/storage bay and a wider but still branch-filled snowy window section. Do not add empty sky or snow.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Exterior snow must be textured and layered.
- Keep shelves from becoming too dark.
- Cabin should feel intimate rather than luxurious.
- Cat should be natural.

**520 master filename:** `hearthside_seasonal-hearths_snowed-in-cabin-library_520_5x4.png`  
**1014 extension filename:** `hearthside_seasonal-hearths_snowed-in-cabin-library_1014_3x2.png`

# Design 2 — Christmas Bookshop After Closing

**Collection:** Seasonal Hearths / Quiet Shops  
**Primary buyer:** book lovers, bookstore lovers, holiday gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `christmas bookstore puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** The last customer left, the snow started, and the shop finally belongs to the books.

## Product Requirement

An independent bookshop closed for the night, decorated with evergreen swags and paper stars. Warm lights illuminate shelves, counter, rolling ladder, wrapped book parcels, and orange tabby while snow falls outside.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- book shelves and rolling ladder
- paper stars and evergreen swags
- counter lamp
- wrapped book parcels
- orange tabby
- snowy storefront
- rug, umbrella stand, ribbon, window display

### Lighting / Palette

Warm pendant and counter lamp; cool snowy storefront contrast. Palette: amber, honeyed wood, forest, cream, muted cranberry, winter blue.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle inside a small independent bookshop after closing on a snowy December night. Warm pendant lights and one shaded counter lamp illuminate tall wooden shelves, a rolling ladder, reading chair, display table, and counter. Tasteful holiday décor includes evergreen swags, paper stars, dried orange slices, a few warm white lights, and small stacks of wrapped books in kraft paper and muted ribbon. An orange tabby sits on the counter. Tall storefront windows show gently falling snow, wet cobblestones, and neighboring warm windows. Add patterned rug, umbrella stand, parcel twine, potted plant, and varied book clusters. Adult storybook book-cover realism, soft brush texture, warm amber against winter blue. No people, no readable book titles or shop sign, no fantasy magic, no neon.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

small independent Christmas bookshop after closing, snowy night, warm pendant lights counter lamp, wooden shelves rolling ladder reading chair display table, evergreen swags paper stars dried orange wrapped books kraft ribbon, orange tabby counter, snowy storefront wet cobbles, painterly realism --ar 5:4 --stylize 190 --no readable signs words fantasy magic neon 3d

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Correct shelf/ladder perspective, cat, gifts, window geometry, rug, furniture, and lights. Break book areas into clear clusters and make paper stars, evergreen, ribbons, cobbles, and snow distinct. Remove generated lettering.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 with more storefront window/display detail on one side and another shelf/reading-chair bay on the other. Preserve counter and ladder focal structure.

### Quality-Control Notes

- No fake storefront lettering.
- Holiday décor should look handmade and restrained.
- Outside street must remain visible through snow.
- Ladder and shelves must be believable.

# Design 3 — Mountain Lodge Puzzle Night

**Collection:** Seasonal Hearths / Cabin Weekends  
**Primary buyer:** puzzle lovers, families, winter-cabin gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `winter cabin puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** A puzzle table inside a lodge while the snow piles up outside.

## Product Requirement

A timber lodge with large in-progress generic jigsaw on the table, sorting trays, cocoa, stone hearth, wool blankets, evergreen, gifts, and windows pressed close with snowy pine boughs.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- in-progress generic landscape puzzle
- sorting trays
- stone hearth
- timber beams
- snowy close-up window branches
- wool blankets
- mugs, cookies, books, gifts, evergreen

### Lighting / Palette

Stone hearth and shaded lamp; cool snowy branches outside. Palette: amber, forest, rust, cream, muted cranberry, winter blue.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle scene of a mountain lodge puzzle night during heavy snow. A large wooden table holds an in-progress generic landscape jigsaw with several sorting trays of pieces, cocoa mugs, and a small plate of cookies. A stone fireplace and shaded lamp provide warm light. Add timber beams, wool blankets, books, evergreen branches, a few wrapped gifts, firewood, and divided windows pressed close with snow-covered pine boughs. The room should feel lived-in and attainable rather than luxury-resort grand. Adult illustrated-book-cover style, visible soft brushwork, rich texture for puzzling, no people, no logos, no branded puzzle box, no readable text.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

mountain lodge puzzle night, heavy snow, wooden table in-progress generic jigsaw sorting trays, cocoa cookies, stone fireplace shaded lamp, timber beams wool blankets books evergreen gifts firewood, windows snowy pine boughs, cozy painterly realism --ar 5:4 --stylize 175 --no people words logos branded puzzle box neon luxury resort

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Keep inner jigsaw generic and simple. Correct piece shapes, trays, table, mugs, fireplace, beams, gifts, and windows. Increase plaid/wool, wood, stone, evergreen, and snow textures. Remove accidental logos or text.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 with more blanket/chair/firewood detail on one side and more snowy branch/window/shelving detail on the other. Keep the table puzzle visually simple.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Avoid confusing recursive detail.
- Actual room must be more detailed than inner puzzle.
- No blank snow field.
- Do not make lodge commercial or oversized.

**520 master filename:** `hearthside_seasonal-hearths_mountain-lodge-puzzle-night_520_5x4.png`  
**1014 extension filename:** `hearthside_seasonal-hearths_mountain-lodge-puzzle-night_1014_3x2.png`
