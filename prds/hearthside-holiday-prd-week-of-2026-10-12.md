## Production Product Decision

### Primary production SKU — Printify

**Product:** Puzzle (110, 252, 520, 1014-piece)  
**Provider:** District Photo  
**Default launch variant:** **520 pcs, Horizontal — 20.00 × 15.98 in**  
**Premium variant:** **1014 pcs, Horizontal — 28.50 × 19.02 in**  
**Material / finish:** 100% chipboard, glossy laminated finish  
**Packaging:** paper box with artwork printed on top; reference print included  
**Current catalog signals:** Bestseller; provider score about 9.3; from $8.39; US shipping from $5.99; avg. production time about 2.2 days at research time.

**Why selected:** the 520-piece horizontal format maps almost perfectly to Hearthside's existing 5:4 horizontal art system, while District Photo offers a 1014-piece adult challenge in the same product family. The printed box is giftable without accepting the “uniform non-edge piece” warning attached to the Printify tin product.

### Backup / comparison SKU — Printful

**Product:** Jigsaw Puzzle  
**Variant:** **520 pieces — 19.74 × 15.74 in**  
**Material:** 0.06 in / 1.53 mm pressed paper chipboard  
**Print / finish:** digital print, semi-gloss  
**Current price:** from $15.25  
**US shipping:** from $5.89  
**Availability:** US only

**Use:** backup supplier and controlled sample comparison. The same 5:4 master artwork should transfer with only template-level adjustment.

### Do not use as the default

Do **not** default Hearthside to the Printful 1000-piece tin or the Printify tin product. The Printify tin explicitly uses uniformly cut non-edge pieces, while Printful's similar tin product currently has multiple reviews complaining about repetitive piece shapes / false fits. Customer puzzle experience takes priority over metal packaging.

## Technical Artwork Deliverables

**520-piece / Printful 520 master**
- horizontal **5:4** composition
- generate at **5000 × 4000 px or larger**
- preserve 5–7% safe breathing room around critical focal objects
- fit final art to the current live provider template

**1014-piece premium variant**
- horizontal **3:2** extended composition
- generate / outpaint at **6000 × 4000 px or larger**
- extend scene left/right; do not simply crop the 5:4 master
- preserve the same focal point and storytelling hierarchy

**Shared Hearthside puzzle rules**
- cozy painterly realism; adult illustrated-book-cover feel
- one dominant warm light source, with restrained cool ambient contrast
- foreground / midground / background layers
- 8–12 identifiable detail clusters
- 3–5 strong texture families
- no giant flat dark walls, skies, floors, water, or snow fields
- no text inside the generated artwork
- no neon, hyper-plastic 3D, or fantasy drift unless explicitly requested
- recurring orange tabby may appear, but should not overwhelm the scene

# Design 1 — Winter Village Bookstore

**Collection:** Seasonal Hearths / Quiet Shops  
**Primary buyer:** book lovers, winter-village collectors, gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `winter village puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** One glowing bookstore on a snowy corner is enough to make the whole street feel warm.

## Product Requirement

Exterior street-corner view of a small bookstore at blue hour after snow. Large windows reveal shelves, lamps, and a tiny orange tabby; outside are textured cobbles, wreath, garland, bicycle, footprints, crates, streetlamp, and neighboring façades.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- brick/stone storefront
- glowing multi-pane display windows with interior detail
- snowy cobblestones and footprints
- wreath and restrained garland
- bicycle, crates, streetlamp
- neighboring façades/windows
- orange tabby visible inside

### Lighting / Palette

Warm amber shop windows against muted blue-hour snow. Streetlamp secondary. Palette: brick/rust, amber, forest, cream, muted blue.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of a small neighborhood bookstore on a snowy winter corner at blue hour. View the warmly lit shop from outside across textured cobblestones. Large multi-pane windows reveal wooden bookshelves, a reading lamp, stacked books, and a small orange tabby inside. The brick-and-stone storefront is decorated with one wreath, restrained evergreen garland, paper stars, and warm window lights. Add a bicycle leaning near the wall, wooden crates, footprints in snow, old streetlamp, drainpipe, neighboring façades, and varied lit windows. Snow should collect on ledges, bikes, branches, and stones so the ground is detailed rather than blank. Adult storybook painterly realism, visible brush texture. No readable store name, no people as focal subjects, no neon, no fantasy.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

snowy winter village bookstore blue hour, exterior street corner, warm multi-pane windows shelves lamp books orange tabby, brick stone storefront wreath restrained garland paper stars, bicycle crates footprints old streetlamp neighboring facades, textured cobbles, cozy painterly realism --ar 5:4 --stylize 190 --no readable signs neon fantasy cartoon Santa

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Correct storefront perspective, windows, bricks, bicycle, streetlamp, cat silhouette, neighboring façades, and snow. Add footprints, cobble variation, ledge snow, branch detail, and differentiated window interiors. Remove signage text.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 with one more neighboring storefront/façade section and a longer textured cobblestone foreground containing footprints, snow edges, and street furniture. Do not add blank street.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Storefront cannot rely on readable text.
- Keep snow textured across surfaces.
- Warm windows need interior detail, not flat yellow.
- This is a quiet street, not a crowded market.

**1014 extension filename:** `hearthside_seasonal-hearths_winter-village-bookstore_1014_3x2.png`

# Design 2 — Snowy Window Seat with Cocoa

**Collection:** Seasonal Hearths  
**Primary buyer:** homebodies, readers, winter gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `winter puzzle adults`  
**Emotional hook:** A winter puzzle that can stay relevant long after Christmas.

## Product Requirement

A January-friendly winter window seat with wool blanket, books, cocoa, candles, pine branches, orange tabby, basket, slippers, and layered snowy neighborhood view—no tree or gifts.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- deep snowy window with close branches
- wool textures and layered cushions
- orange tabby
- books, cocoa, glasses
- pine branches in ceramic vase
- lamp and small candles
- basket, slippers, patterned rug

### Lighting / Palette

Shaded brass lamp dominant; small candles secondary; cool snowy blue exterior. Palette: cream, amber, rust, forest, muted blue, deep brown.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of a quiet winter window seat on a snowy evening, designed to sell through Christmas and into January. A deep built-in seat holds a chunky wool blanket, layered cushions, two books, cocoa mug, and an orange tabby watching the snow. A shaded brass lamp is the main warm light, with two small candles as accents. Place pine branches in a ceramic vase, a woven basket, slippers, reading glasses, and a patterned rug nearby. Outside the divided window, close snow-covered branches and distant blue-gray houses create layered winter detail. Use cream, amber, rust, forest green, muted blue, deep brown. No Christmas tree, no wrapped gifts, no holiday text. Adult storybook painterly realism with visible soft brushwork.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

quiet winter window seat, January-friendly, chunky wool blanket cushions books cocoa orange tabby watching snow, brass lamp warm main light, small candles, pine branches ceramic vase basket slippers glasses patterned rug, snowy branches distant houses, cozy painterly realism --ar 5:4 --stylize 175 --no Christmas tree gifts words neon blank snow

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Correct cat, window geometry, branches, lamp, mug, books, blanket, basket, and rug. Increase differentiation in wool knit, cushion fabrics, rug pattern, snow-covered branches, and distant house shapes. Keep it explicitly winter rather than Christmas and remove pseudo-text.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 with more built-in shelving / basket detail on one side and more layered snowy house/branch view on the other. Keep the winter-not-Christmas positioning intact.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Must work as a January listing unchanged.
- Avoid featureless white snow.
- Keep candles secondary to lamp.
- Textiles should vary but remain harmonious.

# Design 3 — Winter Conservatory with Paper Stars

**Collection:** Seasonal Hearths / Garden Rooms  
**Primary buyer:** plant lovers, winter décor buyers, cozy puzzle fans  
**Search-language seed:** `winter garden puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** Plants, snow, and warm paper stars instead of another red-and-green Christmas scene.

## Product Requirement

A snowy glass conservatory at night with cream paper stars, citrus, rosemary, ferns, terracotta pots, wicker chair, tea table, lantern, orange tabby, patterned tile, and wool blanket.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- snowy glass-roof grid
- hanging cream paper stars
- winter greenery and citrus
- terracotta pots
- wicker chair and wool blanket
- tea table and brass lantern
- orange tabby
- patterned tile, watering can, basket

### Lighting / Palette

Vintage brass lantern dominant; cool frost and snow beyond glass. Palette: amber, forest, terracotta, cream, muted blue.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of a glass conservatory on a snowy winter night. Snow rests along the glass roof frames and outside branches while the panes remain detailed with frost and reflections. Inside, one vintage brass lantern provides the main warm light. Hang several simple cream paper stars in the windows as seasonal décor. Fill the conservatory with restrained winter greenery, a small citrus tree, rosemary, ferns, trailing plants, and terracotta pots. Add a wicker chair with wool blanket, small tea table, ceramic cup, orange tabby, watering can, basket, and patterned tile floor. Adult storybook realism, soft brush texture, rich detail for a 1000-piece puzzle. No people, no text, no giant Christmas tree, no saturated holiday colors.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

snowy winter glass conservatory, glass roof grid snow frost reflections, vintage brass lantern warm light, cream paper stars, citrus rosemary ferns trailing plants terracotta pots, wicker chair wool blanket tea table orange tabby watering can basket patterned tile, cozy painterly realism --ar 5:4 --stylize 195 --no words giant Christmas tree neon candy colors jungle chaos 3d

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Correct glass-frame geometry, paper stars, plants, pots, chair, cat, lantern, tile, and snow. Make each plant species and pot distinct; add frost, roof-frame snow, tile pattern, wicker, knit, and leaf textures. Keep seasonal but January-friendly. Remove accidental text.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 by continuing the conservatory wall and plant benches on both sides with distinct pots/leaves/frost detail. Preserve chair-lantern-cat focal area and avoid blank glass.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Paper stars must look physically plausible.
- Glass geometry must be consistent.
- Keep greens restrained.
- Snow should emphasize structure rather than flatten roof.

**520 master filename:** `hearthside_seasonal-hearths_winter-conservatory-paper-stars_520_5x4.png`  
**1014 extension filename:** `hearthside_seasonal-hearths_winter-conservatory-paper-stars_1014_3x2.png`
