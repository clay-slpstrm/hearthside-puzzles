## Production Product Decision

### Primary production SKU — Printify

**Product:** Puzzle (110, 252, 520, 1014-piece)  
**Provider:** District Photo  
**Default launch variant:** **520 pcs, Horizontal — 20.00 × 15.98 in**  
**Premium variant:** **1014 pcs, Horizontal — 28.50 × 19.02 in**  
**Material / finish:** 100% chipboard, glossy laminated finish  
**Packaging:** paper box with artwork printed on top; reference print included  
**Current catalog signals:** Bestseller; provider score about 9.3; from $8.39; US shipping from $5.99; avg. production time about 2.2 days at research time.

**Why selected:** the 520-piece horizontal format maps almost perfectly to Hearthside's existing 5:4 horizontal art system, while District Photo offers a 1014-piece adult challenge in the same product family. The printed box is giftable without accepting the “uniform non-edge piece” warning attached to the Printify tin product.

### Backup / comparison SKU — Printful

**Product:** Jigsaw Puzzle  
**Variant:** **520 pieces — 19.74 × 15.74 in**  
**Material:** 0.06 in / 1.53 mm pressed paper chipboard  
**Print / finish:** digital print, semi-gloss  
**Current price:** from $15.25  
**US shipping:** from $5.89  
**Availability:** US only

**Use:** backup supplier and controlled sample comparison. The same 5:4 master artwork should transfer with only template-level adjustment.

### Do not use as the default

Do **not** default Hearthside to the Printful 1000-piece tin or the Printify tin product. The Printify tin explicitly uses uniformly cut non-edge pieces, while Printful's similar tin product currently has multiple reviews complaining about repetitive piece shapes / false fits. Customer puzzle experience takes priority over metal packaging.

## Technical Artwork Deliverables

**520-piece / Printful 520 master**
- horizontal **5:4** composition
- generate at **5000 × 4000 px or larger**
- preserve 5–7% safe breathing room around critical focal objects
- fit final art to the current live provider template

**1014-piece premium variant**
- horizontal **3:2** extended composition
- generate / outpaint at **6000 × 4000 px or larger**
- extend scene left/right; do not simply crop the 5:4 master
- preserve the same focal point and storytelling hierarchy

**Shared Hearthside puzzle rules**
- cozy painterly realism; adult illustrated-book-cover feel
- one dominant warm light source, with restrained cool ambient contrast
- foreground / midground / background layers
- 8–12 identifiable detail clusters
- 3–5 strong texture families
- no giant flat dark walls, skies, floors, water, or snow fields
- no text inside the generated artwork
- no neon, hyper-plastic 3D, or fantasy drift unless explicitly requested
- recurring orange tabby may appear, but should not overwhelm the scene

# Design 1 — Lakeside Cabin at Dusk

**Collection:** Cabin Weekends  
**Primary buyer:** cabin lovers, nature lovers, couples, gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `cabin jigsaw puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** The lake has gone quiet and the cabin lights just came on.

## Product Requirement

A rustic cabin living room at blue hour, looking through broad windows to a calm lake, dock, canoe, and layered pines. A stone fireplace, floor lamp, leather chair, plaid, books, mugs, and cabin objects carry the puzzle detail.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- stone hearth and stacked firewood
- leather chair with forest/rust plaid blanket
- divided windows with lake, dock, canoe, pines
- books, folded map without text, binoculars, mugs
- woven basket, beams, wood floorboards
- reflections and shoreline layers to avoid empty water

### Lighting / Palette

Stone hearth plus amber floor lamp; cool lake-blue dusk outside. Palette: amber, forest, rust, deep brown, muted blue, cream.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 Hearthside jigsaw puzzle artwork of a rustic lakeside cabin interior at dusk. View from the living room toward broad divided windows overlooking a calm lake, small dock, tied canoe, and layered pine trees under a muted blue evening sky. Inside, a stone fireplace and one amber floor lamp create the warm glow across a worn leather chair, forest-green and rust plaid blanket, stacked firewood, low table with two mugs, books, folded paper map without legible text, binoculars, woven basket, and wood floorboards. Cozy painterly realism, adult illustrated-book-cover quality, visible soft brush texture, believable cabin architecture, many mid-sized details. No people, no text, no giant empty lake or sky, no dramatic fantasy lighting.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

rustic lakeside cabin interior at blue hour, stone fireplace, amber floor lamp, leather chair, forest rust plaid, stacked firewood, books map binoculars mugs basket, broad windows, lake dock canoe pines, warm amber muted blue, cozy painterly realism adult storybook --ar 5:4 --stylize 180 --no words neon huge blank sky huge empty water fantasy 3d

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Polish the cabin for a jigsaw puzzle. Reduce any flat lake/sky area by strengthening reflections, shoreline layers, pine silhouettes, dock boards, and window divisions. Correct canoe, fireplace stones, chair, floorboards, and objects. Keep fire and lamp warm but detailed rather than blown out.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 with additional cabin wall/shelving on one side and a slightly wider layered lake/pine view on the other. Add detail rather than open water; preserve the hearth-chair focal area.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Exterior should not dominate more than about 35–40% of the scene.
- Window mullions and shoreline detail must break blue areas.
- Fireplace stones should be varied but calm.
- Cabin should feel attainable, not a luxury resort.

# Design 2 — Forest Cabin Reading Loft

**Collection:** Cabin Weekends  
**Primary buyer:** book lovers, cabin lovers, introverts  
**Search-language seed:** `cozy cabin puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** A tiny loft, a stack of books, and nowhere else you need to be.

## Product Requirement

A compact reading loft under a sloped timber roof. A built-in daybed beneath a small pine-forest window is surrounded by books, baskets, a patchwork quilt, a shaded lamp, subtle string lights, and a sleepy orange tabby.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- sloped timber roof and exposed beams
- small layered pine-forest window
- daybed with quilt and cushions
- book shelves and stacked crates
- orange tabby at foot of bed
- woven baskets, slippers, mug, lamp, subtle string lights

### Lighting / Palette

Shaded bedside lamp as dominant light; string lights secondary. Palette: honeyed wood, rust, forest, ochre, muted blue, cream.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of a compact reading loft in a forest cabin at night. The room sits under a sloped timber ceiling with exposed beams. A built-in daybed beneath a small window is covered in layered cream cushions and a patchwork quilt in rust, forest green, muted blue, and ochre. Dense pine branches are visible outside. A shaded bedside lamp is the single dominant warm light; a few dim string-light points add secondary sparkle. Surround the bed with low book shelves, stacked wooden crates of books, woven baskets, slippers, a ceramic mug, folded blanket, and a sleepy orange tabby at the foot. Adult illustrated storybook realism, soft brush texture, intimate but richly detailed, believable architecture. No text, no people, no fantasy, no giant dark ceiling.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

compact forest cabin reading loft, sloped timber ceiling beams, built-in daybed, patchwork quilt rust green muted blue ochre, small window dense pines, warm bedside lamp, subtle string lights, books crates baskets slippers mug orange tabby, cozy painterly realism --ar 5:4 --stylize 185 --no words neon fantasy huge dark ceiling 3d

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Refine for production. Correct roof-beam perspective, bed geometry, books, cat, lamp, and window. Add distinct quilt patches, wood knots, book-color clusters, woven texture, and pine layers so there are no flat regions. Keep string lights subtle and remove pseudo-text.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend the loft horizontally by adding another low storage/bookshelf bay and more sloped-roof detail on one side, plus additional layered pine/window or blanket/basket detail on the other. Preserve the bed-lamp-cat focal group.

### Quality-Control Notes

- No impossible roof lines or floating beams.
- Room should feel cozy, not cramped.
- Pine view needs layered detail.
- Cat should remain small enough that the room is the product.

**520 master filename:** `hearthside_cabin-weekends_forest-cabin-reading-loft_520_5x4.png`

# Design 3 — Rainy Glass Conservatory

**Collection:** Garden Rooms  
**Primary buyer:** plant lovers, gardeners, cottagecore buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `garden room puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** A rainy greenhouse that feels like the quietest room in the house.

## Product Requirement

A glass conservatory attached to an older home during a gentle rain, with wicker chair, tea table, orange tabby, botanically varied plants, terracotta pots, patterned tile, and a brass wall sconce.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- glass roof/window grid with raindrops
- terracotta and ceramic pots in varied sizes
- distinct leaf silhouettes and plant species
- patterned floor tile
- wicker chair with striped cushion
- tea, watering can, gloves, basket, orange tabby

### Lighting / Palette

Vintage brass wall sconce as dominant warm source; cool rainy ambient light through glass. Palette: amber, forest, terracotta, cream, muted blue.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of a glass conservatory on a rainy late afternoon. The conservatory has a believable grid of glass roof panels and windows streaked with rain. A wicker reading chair with a muted striped cushion sits beside a small round tea table. An old brass wall sconce is the main warm light source, reflecting softly on wet glass. Fill the room with distinct houseplants in terracotta and ceramic pots: small citrus tree, trailing vine, fern, broad-leaf plant, herbs, flowering geraniums. Add an orange tabby on the patterned tile floor, a watering can, gardening gloves, folded newspaper with no readable text, and tea cup. Adult storybook painterly realism, soft brush texture, detailed but calm. No people, no text, no tropical neon greens, no giant flat panes.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

rainy glass conservatory, wicker chair striped cushion, small tea table, brass wall sconce warm glow, glass roof grid raindrops, terracotta pots citrus ferns broad leaves trailing vines geraniums herbs, orange tabby, patterned tile, watering can gloves tea, cozy painterly realism --ar 5:4 --stylize 200 --no text neon jungle chaos fantasy 3d

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Correct glass-frame perspective, plant anatomy, pot rims, chair weaving, tile perspective, cat, and watering can. Increase differentiation among plant species and pot colors while keeping the palette muted. Preserve the brass sconce as dominant source and remove generated words.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 by continuing the glass wall and plant bench to each side. Add distinct pots / leaves / rain patterns rather than empty glazing. Preserve chair-table-cat focal triangle.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Plant leaves must be varied, not repetitive AI blobs.
- Window grid must remain geometrically consistent.
- Greens should stay restrained.
- Tile pattern should be legible without optical noise.

**520 master filename:** `hearthside_garden-rooms_rainy-glass-conservatory_520_5x4.png`  
**1014 extension filename:** `hearthside_garden-rooms_rainy-glass-conservatory_1014_3x2.png`
