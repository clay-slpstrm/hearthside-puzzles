**Week of:** September 28, 2026  
**Lane:** Holiday 2026  
**Production goal:** 3 finished puzzle designs / 3 Etsy listings, each ready for 520-piece launch and 1014-piece extension.

## Weekly Commercial Brief

Week 1 establishes Seasonal Hearths for the holiday season with three recognizable at-home moments: reading, baking, and game night. Keep Christmas cues restrained and painterly rather than novelty-driven.

## Production Product Decision

### Primary production SKU — Printify

**Product:** Puzzle (110, 252, 520, 1014-piece)  
**Provider:** District Photo  
**Default launch variant:** **520 pcs, Horizontal — 20.00 × 15.98 in**  
**Premium variant:** **1014 pcs, Horizontal — 28.50 × 19.02 in**  
**Material / finish:** 100% chipboard, glossy laminated finish  
**Packaging:** paper box with artwork printed on top; reference print included  
**Current catalog signals:** Bestseller; provider score about 9.3; from $8.39; US shipping from $5.99; avg. production time about 2.2 days at research time.

**Why selected:** the 520-piece horizontal format maps almost perfectly to Hearthside's existing 5:4 horizontal art system, while District Photo offers a 1014-piece adult challenge in the same product family. The printed box is giftable without accepting the “uniform non-edge piece” warning attached to the Printify tin product.

### Backup / comparison SKU — Printful

**Product:** Jigsaw Puzzle  
**Variant:** **520 pieces — 19.74 × 15.74 in**  
**Material:** 0.06 in / 1.53 mm pressed paper chipboard  
**Print / finish:** digital print, semi-gloss  
**Current price:** from $15.25  
**US shipping:** from $5.89  
**Availability:** US only

**Use:** backup supplier and controlled sample comparison. The same 5:4 master artwork should transfer with only template-level adjustment.

### Do not use as the default

Do **not** default Hearthside to the Printful 1000-piece tin or the Printify tin product. The Printify tin explicitly uses uniformly cut non-edge pieces, while Printful's similar tin product currently has multiple reviews complaining about repetitive piece shapes / false fits. Customer puzzle experience takes priority over metal packaging.

## Technical Artwork Deliverables

**520-piece / Printful 520 master**
- horizontal **5:4** composition
- generate at **5000 × 4000 px or larger**
- preserve 5–7% safe breathing room around critical focal objects
- fit final art to the current live provider template

**1014-piece premium variant**
- horizontal **3:2** extended composition
- generate / outpaint at **6000 × 4000 px or larger**
- extend scene left/right; do not simply crop the 5:4 master
- preserve the same focal point and storytelling hierarchy

**Shared Hearthside puzzle rules**
- cozy painterly realism; adult illustrated-book-cover feel
- one dominant warm light source, with restrained cool ambient contrast
- foreground / midground / background layers
- 8–12 identifiable detail clusters
- 3–5 strong texture families
- no giant flat dark walls, skies, floors, water, or snow fields
- no text inside the generated artwork
- no neon, hyper-plastic 3D, or fantasy drift unless explicitly requested
- recurring orange tabby may appear, but should not overwhelm the scene

# Design 1 — Christmas Eve Reading Nook

**Collection:** Seasonal Hearths  
**Primary buyer:** book lovers, Christmas gift buyers, homebodies  
**Search-language seed:** `christmas reading nook puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** Christmas Eve after the house finally gets quiet.

## Product Requirement

A deep snowy window seat on Christmas Eve with modest natural tree, brass lamp, books, cocoa, wrapped gifts, paper stars, dried-orange garland, and an orange tabby curled in a plaid blanket.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- snowy multi-pane window
- modest natural tree with warm lights
- plaid blanket and layered cushions
- orange tabby
- books, cocoa, glasses
- muted wrapped gifts
- paper stars, dried-orange garland, pine, brass lamp

### Lighting / Palette

Brass table lamp dominates; tree lights secondary; cool blue snow outside. Palette: amber, forest, rust, cream, muted cranberry, winter blue.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 Hearthside jigsaw puzzle of a cozy Christmas Eve reading nook. Show a deep built-in window seat beneath a snow-frosted multi-pane window, layered cream cushions, a rust-and-forest plaid blanket, and an orange tabby curled beside an open book. A brass table lamp is the main warm light source. Nearby stands a modest natural Christmas tree with warm white lights and restrained handmade ornaments, plus a few wrapped gifts in kraft, cream, forest green, and muted red papers. Add bookshelves, cocoa mug, reading glasses, paper stars, dried-orange garland, pine branches, wood trim, and patterned rug. Outside: softly falling snow and dark blue winter trees, broken up by window muntins and branch detail. Cozy painterly realism, adult illustrated-book-cover quality, soft brush texture. No people, no readable text, no cartoon Christmas motifs, no neon, no giant blank snow areas.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Christmas Eve reading nook, snow-frosted window seat, orange tabby, open book, brass lamp warm glow, modest natural Christmas tree warm lights handmade ornaments, kraft cream forest muted red gifts, books cocoa glasses paper stars dried orange garland pine, cozy painterly realism --ar 5:4 --stylize 185 --no words Santa elves neon candy colors 3d huge blank snow

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Correct tree branches, ornaments, gifts, cat, books, window geometry, lamp, and cushions. Make wrapping papers, plaid, books, garland, and snow-covered branches distinct puzzle regions. Keep decorations tasteful and believable. Remove all generated lettering.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend 3:2 by adding more bookshelf / garland detail to one side and more snowy branch / curtain / side-table detail to the other. Keep the cat, lamp, and tree in their existing scale relationship.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Tree should be modest, not half the room.
- Snow view needs trees/branches/window divisions.
- Avoid saturated primary red/green.
- No duplicated ornaments or warped gift boxes.

**520 master filename:** `hearthside_seasonal-hearths_christmas-eve-reading-nook_520_5x4.png`  
**1014 extension filename:** `hearthside_seasonal-hearths_christmas-eve-reading-nook_1014_3x2.png`

An early Christmas morning kitchen under one warm pendant, with blue dawn outside, cinnamon rolls, bread, oranges/cloves, greenery, mugs, copper, checked towels, and a sleepy orange tabby.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- wooden kitchen table
- cinnamon rolls and bread
- oranges, cloves, greenery
- mugs and teapot
- pendant lamp
- blue dawn window
- orange tabby
- checked textiles, jars, copper pans, small parcel

### Lighting / Palette

Pendant lamp dominates; cool blue dawn at window. Palette: amber, cinnamon, forest, cream, copper, deep winter blue.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of a quiet kitchen early on Christmas morning before the household is awake. A wooden worktable sits under one warm shaded pendant lamp, the dominant light. On the table: a tray of cinnamon rolls, fresh bread, oranges with cloves, ceramic mugs, teapot, checked towel, and a small wrapped parcel. The kitchen has cream cabinets, open shelves with jars and dishes, copper pans, evergreen garland, dried orange slices, and a simple wreath. Through the window, blue winter dawn and light snow create cool contrast. An orange tabby sits sleepily on a chair. In a background doorway, only a small glimpse of stockings and tree glow. Adult storybook painterly realism, soft brush texture, muted amber, cinnamon, forest, cream, deep blue. No people, no text, no candy-color holiday styling.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Correct cabinet perspective, pan handles, food, mugs, cat, table, and window. Strengthen checks, shelf groups, bread texture, greenery, copper, wood grain, and dawn snow. Keep holiday décor restrained and remove label text.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Food should remain painterly and appetizing.
- Holiday cues should be obvious but restrained.
- Background tree cannot compete with table.
- Preserve warm-vs-cool separation.

# Design 3 — Holiday Game Night by the Fire

**Collection:** Seasonal Hearths  
**Primary buyer:** families, couples, puzzle lovers, holiday gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `holiday jigsaw puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** The gifts are opened; now the good part of the evening starts.

## Product Requirement

A post-dinner holiday living room with a round table holding a generic wordless game, cocoa, cookies, firelight, a modest tree, stockings, plaid textiles, and snow beyond the windows.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- round table with original unbranded game
- stone fireplace
- modest tree and stockings
- cookies, cocoa, napkins
- plaid chairs and blankets
- snowy windows
- bookcase, firewood, slippers, garland, gifts

### Lighting / Palette

Fireplace is dominant warm source; tree lights secondary; cool snow outside. Palette: amber, forest, rust, cream, muted cranberry, winter blue.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 cozy painterly-realism jigsaw puzzle of holiday game night in a warm living room. The focal point is a round wooden game table with original unbranded illustrated cards containing no words, simple wooden tokens, cocoa mugs, and a plate of cookies. A stone fireplace is the main warm light source, with a modest Christmas tree and stockings in the background. Add plaid throws, upholstered chairs, garland, bookshelves, firewood basket, slippers, wrapped gifts, and divided windows showing softly falling snow. Adult storybook realism, visible soft brush texture, detailed, believable, cozy, screen-free. No people, no logos, no recognizable board game, no readable text, no neon.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

holiday family game night living room, round wooden table generic wordless cards wooden tokens cocoa cookies, stone fireplace warm glow, modest Christmas tree stockings, plaid blankets chairs garland books firewood slippers gifts, snowy windows, cozy painterly realism --ar 5:4 --stylize 180 --no people words logos branded games neon cartoon

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Keep tabletop game fully generic and text-free. Correct table ellipse, cards, tokens, chairs, fireplace stones, tree, stockings, and window geometry. Increase differentiation in plaid, wrapping papers, garland, books, and snow branches. Maintain hearth as main light source.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend to 3:2 with more bookcase / firewood detail at one side and more snowy window / chair / gift detail at the other. Keep the table and hearth dominant.

### Quality-Control Notes

- No copyrighted board-game look.
- Table must read at small size.
- Avoid too many tiny cards/pieces.
- Tree supports the room rather than dominates it.
