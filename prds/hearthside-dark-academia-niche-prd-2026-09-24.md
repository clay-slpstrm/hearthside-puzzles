# Rainy Scholar’s Library

**Collection:** Cozy Rooms — Warm Dark Academia  
**Primary buyer:** readers, students, teachers, dark-academia buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `dark academia library puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** A library that makes a rainy evening feel like an invitation to stay up too late reading.

## Product Requirement

A believable old home library on a rain-heavy evening. A worn leather chair and writing desk sit between tall wood shelves and a multi-pane window. The room feels scholarly, lived-in, and warm—not haunted. A green-shaded brass desk lamp is the dominant light.

## Must-Include Puzzle Details

- tall wood shelves broken into distinct book-color clusters
- rain-streaked multi-pane window with stone or wood trim
- worn leather reading chair with visible creases
- writing desk with notebook, fountain pen, glasses, teacup, and stacked books
- small globe or map roll with no readable text
- patterned rug, firewood basket, plant, brass details
- optional orange tabby sleeping beneath the desk

## Lighting / Palette

Dominant light: green-shaded brass desk lamp with warm amber cast. Secondary: cool rainy blue from the window. Palette: walnut, oxblood, forest green, muted navy, parchment cream, tarnished brass.

## ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Show tall walnut bookshelves, a worn leather armchair, a sturdy writing desk, and a large multi-pane window streaked with rain. A green-shaded brass desk lamp is the single dominant warm light source, illuminating an open notebook with no readable writing, fountain pen, reading glasses, teacup, and several stacked books. Add a small globe, rolled maps without text, patterned rug, plant, firewood basket, brass details, and an optional small orange tabby sleeping beneath the desk. Cool rainy blue outside contrasts with warm amber wood and leather. Cozy painterly realism, adult illustrated-book-cover quality, soft visible brushwork, believable perspective, 8–12 distinct detail clusters, rich puzzle texture. Scholarly and romantic, never spooky. No skulls, no occult symbols, no readable book titles, no fantasy school, no huge black walls, no neon, no 3D render.

**Search-language seed:** `old library jigsaw puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** The campus is quiet, the lamps are still on, and rain is tapping the tall windows.

## Product Requirement

A small historic reading room after hours with two long wood tables, green-shaded lamps, tall windows, book-lined walls, and a few abandoned reading materials. No visible institution branding or people.

## Must-Include Puzzle Details

- two long tables with believable perspective
- several green-shaded reading lamps
- tall rain-darkened windows with stone trim
- bookcases and lower cabinets
- chairs with varied angles
- books, notebooks, glasses, pencils, tea mug
- radiator, umbrella stand, patterned runner, wall clock without legible numerals if distorted

Dominant light: pool of warm light from several matching green-shaded table lamps. Secondary: cool rainy exterior. Palette: deep wood, bottle green, burgundy, cream stone, muted blue.
