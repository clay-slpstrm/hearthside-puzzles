## Shared commercial direction

This is the most time-sensitive new lane. Build 3–6 differentiated winter-village puzzles now, using Hearthside warm-window realism rather than flat folk-art imitation. Keep two designs generic-winter enough to sell through January.

**Lead keyword seeds:** `winter village puzzle`, `Christmas village puzzle`, `Nordic puzzle`, `Scandinavian puzzle`, `snowy village jigsaw`, `cozy winter puzzle`

**Default art direction:** Cozy painterly realism, like an illustrated book cover for adults, with visible soft brush texture. Rich but gentle Hearthside palette of warm amber, rust, deep brown, forest green, muted blue and cream. Use one clear warm light source and soft inviting shadows. Compose in horizontal 5:4 with a clear focal point, layered foreground/midground/background, 8–12 distinct mid-sized detail clusters, and varied puzzle-friendly textures. Avoid huge flat areas, neon, candy saturation, flat vector art, plastic 3D, fantasy spectacle, random pseudo-text, readable brand names, and watermarks.

**Primary search phrase:** `winter village puzzle`  
**Primary SKU:** Printify District Photo 520-piece horizontal  
**Upsell variant:** Printify District Photo 1014-piece horizontal

## Core concept

A quiet Nordic-inspired village lane at blue hour during the first snowfall. The focal building is a small warmly lit bookshop with multi-pane windows revealing shelves and lamps; neighboring bakery and homes add varied windows, wreaths and textures. Snow clings to roofs, bicycles, crates, stone steps and branches.

## 1014-piece extension instruction

For the 1014-piece 3:2 edition, extend the approved 5:4 composition **laterally** rather than cropping it. Add one natural scene zone on each side (shelf/window/furniture/storefront/garden/cabin detail as appropriate), preserving the original focal point, lighting direction, scale and perspective. The extension must feel like more of the same believable room or street, not a larger commercial space or a new focal scene.

## Listing angle

Lead with the scene-specific phrase rather than generic “cozy puzzle.” Position it as an adult 520/1014-piece screen-free gift for the niche buyer, with the Hearthside slow-evening story secondary. Use the strongest niche phrase naturally in the first half of the title and one or two close variants in tags.
