**Week of:** September 28, 2026  
**Lane:** Evergreen  
**Production goal:** 3 finished puzzle designs / 3 Etsy listings, each ready for 520-piece launch and 1014-piece extension.

## Weekly Commercial Brief

Week 1 establishes the strongest evergreen Hearthside identity: bookish rooms and a lived-in kitchen. The goal is three highly distinct thumbnails that still look like chapters from the same cozy world.

## Production Product Decision

### Primary production SKU — Printify

**Product:** Puzzle (110, 252, 520, 1014-piece)  
**Provider:** District Photo  
**Default launch variant:** **520 pcs, Horizontal — 20.00 × 15.98 in**  
**Premium variant:** **1014 pcs, Horizontal — 28.50 × 19.02 in**  
**Material / finish:** 100% chipboard, glossy laminated finish  
**Packaging:** paper box with artwork printed on top; reference print included  
**Current catalog signals:** Bestseller; provider score about 9.3; from $8.39; US shipping from $5.99; avg. production time about 2.2 days at research time.

**Why selected:** the 520-piece horizontal format maps almost perfectly to Hearthside's existing 5:4 horizontal art system, while District Photo offers a 1014-piece adult challenge in the same product family. The printed box is giftable without accepting the “uniform non-edge piece” warning attached to the Printify tin product.

### Backup / comparison SKU — Printful

**Product:** Jigsaw Puzzle  
**Variant:** **520 pieces — 19.74 × 15.74 in**  
**Material:** 0.06 in / 1.53 mm pressed paper chipboard  
**Print / finish:** digital print, semi-gloss  
**Current price:** from $15.25  
**US shipping:** from $5.89  
**Availability:** US only

**Use:** backup supplier and controlled sample comparison. The same 5:4 master artwork should transfer with only template-level adjustment.

### Do not use as the default

Do **not** default Hearthside to the Printful 1000-piece tin or the Printify tin product. The Printify tin explicitly uses uniformly cut non-edge pieces, while Printful's similar tin product currently has multiple reviews complaining about repetitive piece shapes / false fits. Customer puzzle experience takes priority over metal packaging.

## Technical Artwork Deliverables

**520-piece / Printful 520 master**
- horizontal **5:4** composition
- generate at **5000 × 4000 px or larger**
- preserve 5–7% safe breathing room around critical focal objects
- fit final art to the current live provider template

**1014-piece premium variant**
- horizontal **3:2** extended composition
- generate / outpaint at **6000 × 4000 px or larger**
- extend scene left/right; do not simply crop the 5:4 master
- preserve the same focal point and storytelling hierarchy

**Shared Hearthside puzzle rules**
- cozy painterly realism; adult illustrated-book-cover feel
- one dominant warm light source, with restrained cool ambient contrast
- foreground / midground / background layers
- 8–12 identifiable detail clusters
- 3–5 strong texture families
- no giant flat dark walls, skies, floors, water, or snow fields
- no text inside the generated artwork
- no neon, hyper-plastic 3D, or fantasy drift unless explicitly requested
- recurring orange tabby may appear, but should not overwhelm the scene

# Design 1 — Rainy Window Reading Nook

**Collection:** Cozy Rooms  
**Primary buyer:** book lovers, homebodies, cozy-gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `reading nook puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** A room for people who would happily cancel plans for a book, rain, and tea.

## Product Requirement

Create a deep built-in reading nook in an older, well-kept home on a rainy evening. The focal area is an orange tabby curled beside an open book beneath a brass lamp; built-in shelves, textiles, rain, and small domestic objects provide the puzzle detail.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- rain-streaked multi-pane window with visible droplets and reflections
- built-in bookshelves with varied spine-height/color groups
- patchwork blanket with 4–5 distinct fabric patterns
- orange tabby curled naturally beside an open book
- ceramic mug, reading glasses, bookmark, small plant, wood trim
- patterned rug and cushion seams for edge-to-edge piece differentiation

### Lighting / Palette

Dominant light: shaded brass table lamp. Secondary ambient light: cool rainy blue from the window. Palette: amber, rust, forest green, muted blue, cream, deep brown.

### ChatGPT Image Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

Create a horizontal 5:4 jigsaw-puzzle artwork of a cozy rainy-window reading nook at dusk. Show a deep built-in window seat beneath a large multi-pane window covered in rain streaks, an orange tabby curled beside an open book, layered cream and rust cushions, and a patchwork blanket in forest green, muted blue, rust, and warm cream. A brass table lamp is the single dominant warm light source, glowing across nearby books and wood while cool rainy blue light comes through the window. Fill the room with puzzle-friendly mid-sized details: built-in bookshelves, varied book-spine color groups, ceramic mug, reading glasses, bookmark ribbon, trailing plant, wood trim, patterned rug, fabric folds, and subtle framed art without legible text. Cozy painterly realism, adult illustrated-book-cover quality, soft visible brush texture, believable perspective, gentle shadows, rich but restrained color. Clear focal point at cat and lamp. No people, no text, no neon, no giant flat dark areas, no hyperreal 3D.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

cozy painterly realism, rainy window reading nook at dusk, deep built-in window seat, orange tabby sleeping beside open book, brass lamp single warm light, rain-streaked multi-pane window, built-in bookshelves, patchwork blanket, cream rust forest green muted blue palette, mug glasses plants patterned rug wood grain, adult storybook book-cover painting, soft brush texture, detailed 1000-piece-friendly scene --ar 5:4 --stylize 180 --no words text logo neon 3d render empty walls huge dark areas

### Nano Banana / Image-Editor Refinement Prompt

Refine this as premium Hearthside puzzle art. Preserve the believable rainy reading nook and orange tabby, but improve piece-by-piece visual variety: clearer book-spine groups, distinct patchwork squares, straight window muntins, readable mug/glasses silhouettes, natural wood grain, and separate plant leaves. Correct warped books, cat anatomy, cushions, and window geometry. Remove all pseudo-text. Keep the brass lamp as the dominant warm source.

### 1014-Piece 3:2 Extension Instruction

Extend the 5:4 scene laterally to a 3:2 canvas. Add believable continuation of the bookshelf on one side and more rainy window / curtain / plant detail on the other. Do not enlarge the cat or crop the lamp. Keep the original focal triangle intact.

### Quality-Control Notes

- Cat anatomy must be natural and secondary to the room.
- Rain should add texture without turning the window into a flat blue rectangle.
- Books must not contain generated lettering.
- Lamp glow must preserve local detail.

# Design 2 — Hidden Bookshop After Closing

**Collection:** Cozy Rooms / future Quiet Shops  
**Primary buyer:** bookworms, bookstore lovers, literary gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `cozy bookstore puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** The best part of the bookstore begins after the door is locked.

## Product Requirement

A tiny independent neighborhood bookshop after closing on a rainy night. Warm pendant lights illuminate overflowing wooden shelves, a rolling ladder, reading chair, stacks of books, and an orange tabby on the counter while wet-street reflections glow outside.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- shelves at multiple depths with distinct book clusters
- rolling ladder with believable rails/rungs
- counter with mug, parcels, shaded lamp, orange tabby
- rainy storefront windows with street reflections
- patterned rug, umbrella stand, potted plant
- small display tables and wrapped book parcels with no text

### Lighting / Palette

Warm amber pendant lights plus one counter lamp; cool blue street reflections outside. Palette: honeyed wood, amber, forest, muted navy, cream, rust.

Warm amber pendant lights and the counter lamp provide the interior glow; cool blue rainy street light reflects through tall front windows. Include shelf edges, book clusters, rug pattern, wood floorboards, potted plants, umbrella stand, stacked parcels, window raindrops, and subtle framed prints with no legible text. Adult illustrated-book-cover feeling, visible soft brush strokes, warm and believable, detailed but organized. No people, no readable signage, no fantasy magic, no neon, no huge flat areas.

### Midjourney Prompt — 520-piece 5:4 Master

### Quality-Control Notes

- Window reflections should not obscure interior detail.

**Collection:** Cozy Rooms  
**Primary buyer:** bakers, homebodies, family-gift buyers  
**Search-language seed:** `cozy kitchen puzzle`  
**Emotional hook:** The kitchen after the first tray comes out of the oven.

## Product Requirement

A warm farmhouse-style kitchen on a slow rainy Sunday. A wooden worktable holds cooling bread and cinnamon buns, mixing tools, apples, tea, and an open cookbook; shelves, tile, copper, herbs, and an orange tabby create layered puzzle texture.

### Must-Include Puzzle Details

- wooden worktable with distinct object clusters
- checked towel and patterned oven mitt
- bread and cinnamon buns
- open cookbook with no readable text
- shelves of jars and ceramics
- rainy window, herb pots, copper pan, basket, orange tabby

### Lighting / Palette

Single warm pendant over the table; cool rainy daylight through the window. Palette: cream, amber, cinnamon, forest, muted blue, copper.

In the midground include cream cabinets, open shelves with jars and mismatched ceramics, a hanging copper pan, herb pots, and an orange tabby sitting on a chair. A rain-streaked window provides cool muted-blue contrast. Rich textures in wood grain, tile, fabric, bread crust, ceramics, leaves, and metal. Adult storybook realism, soft brush texture, inviting and frame-worthy, detailed enough for 500–1000 pieces without clutter. No people, no legible text, no neon, no sterile modern kitchen.
