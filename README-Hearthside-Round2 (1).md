## What changed in this research round

This pass goes deeper than the first niche package by validating the existing customer-aesthetic lanes against current public Etsy competition and visible review/price signals, then looking for adjacent phrases that are narrow enough to test while still fitting Hearthside's warm, believable, painterly interior world.

### Existing niches retained

1. **Bookish & Reader Gifts** — strongest core lane.
2. **Warm Dark Academia** — strong differentiation at moderate public competition.
3. **Domestic Cottagecore** — proven demand, but only worth entering through narrower domestic scenes.
4. **Hygge & Cabincore** — strong emotional fit; use cabin/Nordic phrases for discovery.

### New adjacent niches discovered

5. **Cozy Cafés & Tea Rooms** — narrow exact-match competition and visible review evidence; excellent Hearthside fit.
6. **Conservatories & Garden Shops** — greenhouse/flower-shop intersections around ~180–190 public results; very puzzle-friendly.
7. **Craft Rooms, Sewing & Quilting** — buyer-identity gift niche with unusually strong Hearthside texture fit.
8. **Winter Village & Nordic Holiday** — highest immediate seasonal priority because current eRank reporting shows Christmas already near the top of Etsy search demand.

## Production priority for the next 24 designs

**Immediate October / holiday:** Winter Village & Nordic Holiday → Bookish → Hygge/Cabincore.  
**Next evergreen tests:** Cozy Cafés → Warm Dark Academia → Conservatory/Garden Shops.  
**Selective expansion:** Domestic Cottagecore → Craft Rooms/Quilting.

## Cross-cutting motif decision

Do **not** create a separate Cat niche yet. Continue using the orange tabby as a recognizable Hearthside character across Bookish, Cottagecore, Cabincore, Garden and Craft designs. Cat demand is broad, but the brand advantage comes from “cat inside a room I want to inhabit,” not generic cat art.
